FROM THE FRONTIER

One of AI's most respected voices just published his key insights from 2025 — Here’s the TL:DR.

Made with Midjourney

Andrej Karpathy's 2025 LLM Year in Review has racked up 2.2 million views — with four major takeaways you should know.

There’s a new training recipe for LLMs. Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) emerged as a breakthrough new way to train LLMs. The training system works by teaching LLMs how to reason, without constant human feedback. Karpathy estimates that this change — not bigger model sizes — powered most of 2025’s capability gains.

AI is a genius…and an idiot. LLMs are extremely capable where rewards are verifiable, but remain shockingly easy to trick elsewhere. This result: AI that can solve PhD-level math problems, but gets fooled by a simple trick question. Karpathy calls this "jagged intelligence” and cites it as one of the reasons why he’s losing faith in benchmarks.

Vibe coding has changed who can build software. 2025 marked the year that barriers to coding were removed almost entirely. Vibe coding empowers regular people to start writing software and programming professionals to write a lot more. Karpathy estimates that this shift will end up benefiting regular people much more than corporations or governments.

Claude and Gemini separate themselves. Karpathy called Google Gemini Nano Banana “one of the most incredible, paradigm-shifting models of 2025” and Claude Code “the first convincing demonstration of what an LLM agent looks like.” But while these models have made massive strides, he believes the industry has still only achieved 10% of its potential.
