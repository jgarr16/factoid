FROM THE FRONTIER

America is considering a ban on open weight Chinese models — but tech leaders are rallying behind them

Made with Midjourney

Rallying behind open-weight AI. It’s not often that someone’s first social media post gets 60M views. But that’s what happened to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang on Friday. The post was a public letter explaining why open models matter. The statement joins hundreds of others speaking out against a potential ban on open weight Chinese models.

The argument against open weight. The primary reason to ban open weight models is safety. In recent months, open weight models — which anyone can download, run, and modify — have started to match the performance of top frontier ones. Once an open model is released, anyone can use it however they choose with essentially no guardrails. But many tech leaders think this risk is worth the reward.

The argument supporting open weight. At least 25 tech firms have publicly voiced support for open weight models, including Nvidia, Microsoft, IBM, and Dell. Their reasoning is that open weight AI creates competition that drives down costs and distributes AI’s benefits broadly across the economy. Without open weight models, AI could become concentrated among just a handful of frontier labs.

Defining the decade ahead. The final decision will play a major role in how AI and society develop over the coming years. It will also heavily influence the initial public offerings of Anthropic and OpenAI, both of which are planning to list as soon as this year. Notably, Anthropic is the only major AI lab that has not endorsed the letter.

How to clean and prepare messy data with ChatGPT

  • Upload your CSV or spreadsheet into ChatGPT

  • Use a prompt like the one below to clean up your data

“Clean @file name.csv

What's wrong:

  • dates are mixed between MM/DD/YYYY and YYYY-MM-DD

  • currency values include $, commas, and blank cells

  • a few duplicate customer rows came from repeated exports

  • region and category names use several aliases

  • there are pasted summary rows mixed into the data

What I want:

  • write a cleaned CSV and keep the original file unchanged

  • use one date format

  • keep blank currency cells blank

  • preserve source row IDs when possible

  • add a short data-quality note with rows you changed, removed, or could not clean confidently“ —

  • Wait for ChatGPT to generate the cleaned CSV

  • Review the data-quality note to see every change, removal, or flagged row

  • Download the cleaned CSV and use it for analysis, reporting, or importing into other tools

PROMPT

Design a Customer Feedback System

“ChatGPT Prompt: You are an operations and customer insight strategist.

Design a customer feedback system for the business below.

Context:

  • Business type: [business type]

  • Customer journey: [journey]

  • Existing feedback channels: [channels]

  • Team size and owners: [team]

  • Key decisions this system should inform: [decisions]

  • Constraints: [constraints]

Instructions:

  1. Recommend what feedback to collect at which moments in the customer journey.

  2. Propose a lightweight workflow for tagging, triaging, reviewing, and prioritizing feedback.

  3. Clarify ownership, review cadence, and escalation rules.

  4. Show how the system should feed product, service, or operational decisions.

  5. Include a practical loop-closing method when customers should be updated or acknowledged.

Missing information policy: Ask up to 2 concise questions only if the business or customer journey is unclear. Otherwise proceed with explicit assumptions.“ —
