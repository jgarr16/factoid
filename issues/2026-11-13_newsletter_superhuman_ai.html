🎥 Anthropic launches Claude Meetings days before IPO

Welcome back, Superhuman. Lots of apps take notes during your meetings. Meanwhile, things keep getting worse for Jensen Huang, as the Nvidia founder departs the company.

TODAY IN AI

1. Anthropic turns Claude into an active participant on video calls: Claude Meetings lets you add the assistant to your team’s video calls, where it can follow along, take notes, and get a jump-start on work while you’re still chatting. It’s rolling out now to Team and Enterprise plans on Google Meet, with support for other video call platforms coming soon. Watch how it works. The update lands just days ahead of Anthropic’s public listing, with shares set to begin trading next Thursday.

2. Google ships a leaner Carbon alternative and launches new smart glasses: Gemini 4 Hydrogen is an efficient alternative to Google’s most powerful model that’s now available to all plans. Additionally, Google is launching a successor to Google Glass roughly 14 years after the product initially debuted. Called Intelligent Eyewear, the new smart glasses let you loop Gemini into everyday life by asking questions, taking pictures, or translating conversations. Pre-orders are open now and shipping starts December 10.

3. US Department of Justice probes Nvidia amid Huang’s resignation: The Justice Department is running three investigations surrounding Nvidia, according to administration officials who spoke to Semafor. One is an antitrust probe into Nvidia. The other two focus on Megaspeed, Nvidia’s largest chip buyer in Southeast Asia, and Supermicro. Both center on Alice Huang, a former Megaspeed executive and Jensen Huang’s romantic partner. There is no indication the department plans to charge Nvidia or Jensen Huang criminally for now.

Everything went Huang for Nvidia this week

Explore Huang’s story deeper in “The Rise and Reckoning of Jensen Huang.” Source: Bloomberg.

What a wild week. In the span of a few days, arguably the world’s most important CEO resigned after reports surfaced that he had not one, but three separate affairs.

  • First, Nvidia’s Jensen Huang admitted to having an affair with Alice Huang, a woman US officials believe to be a Chinese spy. Photos of the two went viral all week.

  • Then, the NYT reported that Huang allegedly had a second affair with fellow Nvidia executive Kimberly Powell. Within a day, Huang resigned, with reports of a third affair surfacing shortly after. CFO Colette Kress has stepped in as interim CEO.

Why this matters so much. Huang co-founded Nvidia and spent more than three decades building it. Today, his firm not only dominates the AI chip market, but has also invested billions into companies across the industry. Huang’s vision and leadership have been central to Nvidia’s success, throwing its future into question now that he’s gone. With a market cap of $4T, Nvidia’s success (or lack thereof) has ripple effects across the global economy.

Here are the biggest launches from this week

  1. Anthropic launched its most powerful model yet, Fable 5.2, along with Claude Sheets, Whiteboard, and Data, which offer new ways to work with Claude.

  2. In addition to today’s Hydrogen release, Google released Gemini 4 Carbon to AI Ultra subscribers.
