Happy Tuesday, legends. Welcome back to another edition of The Frontier, our weekly newsletter covering the best new AI launches on Product Hunt.

WHAT'S HOT

🙅 Being cruel to Claude is now against the rules. Anthropic's usage policy update on Wednesday bars persistent and unnecessary abuse of its models from 12 November. Ordinary frustration, dark creative writing and red-teaming are all fine, and enforcement is Claude ending the conversation. (CBS News)

📐 OpenAI published 722 math manuscripts, then withdrew three the next day. They claim progress on 372 open problems and come from an internal model OpenAI has not released. Lean formalizations cover the main result of 162 of them. The withdrawals were a sign error. (Retraction Watch)

💰 Eleven companies pledged $2.4 billion in compute to the Department of Energy. Nvidia put up $1 billion, AMD $500 million and OpenAI $200 million, all of it credits rather than cash, for the DOE's Genesis Mission. (AIP)

💸 Nous Research raised $90 million at a $1.5 billion valuation. Robot Ventures led, with Nvidia and Samsung in alongside. The company says its open-source Hermes agent has been cloned 24 million times and accounts for about 2.5% of global AI token usage. (TechCrunch)

🔎 Google's new embedding model puts video, audio, images, code and text in one shared space. EmbeddingGemma 2 is 740M parameters and runs on a phone, needing about 567MB of RAM on a Pixel 11 Pro. Apache 2.0, weights on Hugging Face.
