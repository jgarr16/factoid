FROM THE FRONTIER

Need a starting point for AI? Try letting it handle the “work around the work”

Made with Midjourney

The data behind Anthropic’s update. Anthropic turned Claude Cowork into a cross-device platform on Tuesday, but the announcement buried an interesting takeaway: 50% of the work that Claude Cowork handles comes from just two categories. Anthropic calls it: the work around the work.

How people use Claude Cowork. Anthropic analyzed 1.2M anonymized sessions from May, and found that business operations — tasks like building trackers, pulling scattered updates into a report, or making onboarding checklists — made up 33% of all usage. The runner-up (16%) was content creation, or drafting decks, posts, and proposals. The pattern: these are tasks that need to get done at nearly every company, but rarely belong to any one person's job description.

The takeaway. The most common use cases for Claude Cowork involve tasks that track and communicate information across teams. If you find yourself doing or assigning work like this, then it may be worth handing that task to AI — freeing you and your team to tackle higher-level work.

The repositioning sends a message. Saying that Claude Cowork handles "work around the work" positions the tool as an agentic background worker, not a full end-to-end replacement. It’s a notable continuation of major AI labs walking back doom talk when it comes to AI’s role in the office.

How to give your AI agent a real email inbox (no Gmail required)

  • Install the Nylas CLI and authenticate it using your API key.

  • Create a dedicated email address for your AI agent: nylas agent account create__ __support@acme.nylas.email

  • Add an app password so your agent can send and receive email via IMAP/SMTP: nylas agent account update support@acme.nylas.email --app-password "ValidAgentPass123ABC!"

  • Send a test email to confirm the inbox is working.

  • Open the inbox to verify the email was sent successfully.

  • Check the agent status to make sure everything is running correctly.

Or do it all with one prompt: "Create a Nylas agent account called support@acme.nylas.email, add an app password, send a test email confirming it's live, then check the agent status."
