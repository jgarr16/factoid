FROM THE FRONTIER

AI’s role in science continues to grow as Anthropic hands Claude the keys to physical research equipment

Made with Midjourney

Anthropic just launched the Model Hardware Standard, a system letting Claude operate physical hardware in science labs (watch it control a microscope in this video). The goal is to accelerate scientific discovery through rapid iteration, since AI can propose, run, and learn from experiments much faster than human researchers. That thesis may sound familiar.

“Automating discovery” is the core mission statement behind Discovery Loop, a new startup founded by three of the most-cited researchers in AI, including former Google chief scientist Jeff Dean. Automating the research process — or the “loops of discovery” — is already fueling breakthroughs.

Generating breakthroughs across scientific domains

  • Weather Forecasting: Google DeepMind deployed WeatherNext 2, an AI weather forecaster that’s 8x faster than its predecessor, generating six-hour forecasts four times a day (explore the weather systems in your area here).

  • Biology: A Stanford assistant professor designed 16 previously unknown viruses that kill bacteria.

  • Mathematics: Models from Anthropic and OpenAI have both tackled open conjectures that saw minimal progress for decades.

But while AI has made research easier than ever, humans still play a critical role — pointing that firepower at the problems that matter.

TUTORIAL

How to turn Figma mockups into polished UI with ChatGPT

  • Go to ChatGPT and switch to the Work tab at the top of your screen

  • Click Plugins → Connect plugins → Browse all plugins

  • Search for and install Figma plugin

  • Open the code project where you want to build the design

  • In Figma, select the exact frame, screen, or component you want to turn into code

Copy the link to that specific Figma selection and use the prompt below in ChatGPT:

Sample Prompt: Implement this Figma design in the current project using the @Figma plugin: [Insert Figma Link]. First, pull the design context, assets, variables, and screenshot for the exact frame. Inspect the existing repository and reuse its components, design tokens, typography, spacing, icons, routing, and styling patterns. Make the interface responsive on desktop and mobile. Once the first version is complete, run the project, open the live page, compare it visually with the original Figma frame, and continue correcting the layout, spacing, typography, sizing, colors, and interactions until they closely match. Do not install Tailwind or introduce a new styling system unless the repository already uses it.

  • ChatGPT will analyze the design, build it inside your project, and reuse your existing components

  • Review the result and ask ChatGPT to fix any remaining layout, spacing, or responsiveness issues
