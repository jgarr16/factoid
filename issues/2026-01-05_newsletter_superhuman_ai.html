FROM THE FRONTIER

OpenAI locked up 40% of the world's memory supply — and it’s creating a hidden tax on electronics

Made with Midjourney

OpenAI’s DRAM deal. In October, OpenAI secured preliminary agreements with Samsung and SK Hynix for 900,000 DRAM wafers per month — roughly 40% of global output. OpenAI needs this DRAM (the working memory inside most electronics) to power its data center initiatives, including the $500 billion Stargate project. The impact of this massive deal is already playing out.

Repricing the memory market. OpenAI’s deal has sparked a shortage that’s causing memory prices to skyrocket. A DDR5-5600 64GB memory kit that cost $180 last May. It’s now $710, a nearly 300% increase. Experts expect prices to keep rising in 2026, with Tom’s Guide calling the situation “RAMageddon.”

The ripple effect. Prices aren’t just rising for computer memory kits, but for everything that uses them. While tech giants like Apple or Samsung may absorb the rising costs in the short term, we’ll likely start to see even higher prices and scaled-back devices if this issue persists.

But memory giants aren't standing still. SK Hynix is investing $500 billion in four new fabs, with the first scheduled to come online in 2027. Samsung and Micron are also ramping capacity to meet the demand for AI. Relief won't arrive overnight, but the investment cycle has begun.

PROMPT STATION

Universal Problem Solver

Prompt: You are an expert problem-solver with deep domain knowledge and first-principles thinking.

My problem is: [clearly state the problem or goal]

Constraints & context:

Who this is for: [audience / user]

What success looks like: [specific outcome]

Constraints: [time, budget, tools, skills, risks]

Your task:
Restate the problem in simple terms to confirm understanding.
Identify the real underlying problem (not just the symptoms).
List the key assumptions and challenge the weak ones.
Break the problem into clear, solvable sub-problems.
Propose 2– 3 strong solution paths, with pros/cons.
Recommend the best solution and explain why.
Give a step-by-step action plan I can execute immediately.
Highlight common mistakes to avoid.
