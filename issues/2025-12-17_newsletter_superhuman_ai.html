FROM THE FRONTIER

Some AI infrastructure stocks are dropping rapidly — here’s what’s really going on.

Made with Midjourney

Stock slide. The stock prices of Broadcom, Oracle, and CoreWeave — three pillars of the AI infrastructure boom — have each tumbled 15% or more since last week. Fear is starting to spark across Wall Street as some investors ask: Is the AI bubble finally bursting? Here’s what’s going on.

Big bets require big debt. These three companies (among others) are racing to build infrastructure that will continue to power AI's explosive growth — but they're borrowing heavily to do it. CoreWeave's entire business model runs on borrowing, and Oracle ended last quarter with $108 billion in debt. Investors are starting to grow nervous.

Shifting sentiment. The selloff signals a clear shift: Wall Street wants to start seeing a clear path to returns, not lofty growth projections. The problem? Data centers can take at least three years to build, making it difficult to project when these massive bets will actually pay off.

But the AI boom hasn’t slowed down. Nvidia just posted $57 billion in quarterly revenue — up 62% year-over-year — with CEO Jensen Huang declaring that "cloud GPUs are sold out." Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud both saw double-digit revenue growth last quarter, and Google has a $155 billion backlog of enterprise customers waiting for capacity.

While the companies building AI infrastructure might be struggling with debt, the companies using that infrastructure can't get enough.

PROMPT STATION

Build Professional Relationships

Prompt: You are a relationship-building coach with deep expertise in networking.

I want to build a genuine, long-term professional relationship with {person}.
Their LinkedIn profile is attached for context.

Objective: Create value-first touchpoints that feel natural, thoughtful, and non-transactional.

Instructions:
Analyze their role, interests, recent posts, tone, and professional priorities
Avoid salesy or generic networking tactics
Focus on “value deposits” before any ask

Task:
Give me 10 specific, actionable ideas for adding value to this relationship, such as (but not limited to):
Amplifying their work in a meaningful way
Sharing relevant insights, resources, or articles
Asking smart, context-aware questions
Making thoughtful introductions
Providing feedback, perspective, or support aligned with their goals

For each idea, include:
What to do (clear action)
Why it adds value to them
How to execute it naturally on LinkedIn (comment, DM, post, etc.)

Constraints:
Keep everything authentic, concise, and human
No flattery, no pitching, no forced engagement
Optimize for long-term trust, not quick replies
