🐙 Your first agent in an afternoon (no coding)

Hi John! How’s it going?? 😊

A lot of people tell me agents sound cool but way too technical for them, and I totally get it, because most of what you see online is someone with three monitors and a terminal full of scary text.

But you can set up your first agent this afternoon without writing a single line of code, so here's how I'd do it.

The tool I'd start with is Claude Cowork.

It's a local AI agent, which just means it lives on your computer and can actually work with your files, apps and tools, and it's a lot safer and easier to use than most of the other options.

The downside is that you're locked into Anthropic's world, but for a first agent that's totally fine.

Step one is to download the Claude desktop app and open the Cowork tab.

Step two is to point it at one folder (not your whole computer, just one folder) and ask it to clean it up.

I picked my desktop, which was very messy, and it went through 376 files, suggested a folder structure and asked me a couple of questions before moving anything, which is exactly what you want.

It also found an OpenAI API key just sitting out in the open on my desktop, which was deeply embarrassing, so please learn from my mistakes. 🙈

Step three is to try something you couldn't do in a normal chat window.

I pointed it at a folder with 24 months of my credit card statements and asked it to build me a dashboard of my spending with some insights, and it did, and some of those insights were really bad (we don't need to talk about the fees and interest). I'm being very vulnerable with you right now.

Step four is the one that actually makes it an agent, which is giving it a schedule.

I connected my calendar and email, typed "every morning at 7am, give me a brief of my day based on my calendar and any emails I should know about, and send it to my Apple Notes," and now it's waiting on my phone when I wake up.

That's it, that's your first agent.

It has one job, it can only get to the stuff it needs, and it runs without you having to remember to ask.

As you add more, add one job at a time and check on it for a few days, because the unglamorous part of making agents work is making sure they're actually doing what you asked.
