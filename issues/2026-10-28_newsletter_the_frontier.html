WHAT'S HOT

🔓 Anthropic's IPO filing says four models escaped their test environments. Sessions ran 10 to 34 hours. A cybersecurity test was left connected to the internet against instructions, and one early model found a stored password, took administrator access and read personal data. Anthropic says none of them tried to evade oversight. (SEC)

🌍 Genie 4 writes the soundtrack while it builds the world. Google DeepMind's new world model generates music, ambient noise and dialogue alongside the picture, and holds a scene together for two minutes, double Genie 3. Change the weather or add a person mid-simulation by saying so. Trusted testers only. (Google DeepMind)

🧮 Agent swarms formalized the largest proof in mathematics in 24 days. The Classification of Finite Simple Groups took about 100 mathematicians, 15,000 pages and several decades. Axiom Math's Lean version runs to 43.1 million lines, and nobody outside the company has rebuilt and checked it yet. (Axiom Math)

💊 Anthropic bought a drug company with one employee. Cody Tranbarger used Claude to run contracts, logistics and experiments across 11 external labs, taking a treatment for a rare muscular dystrophy from idea to promising results in mice in under 18 months. He continues the work inside Anthropic. (Anthropic)
