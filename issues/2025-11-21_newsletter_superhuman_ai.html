FROM THE FRONTIER

Is AI a bubble? The answer is murkier than ever.

Made with Midjourney

Nvidia’s earnings report eased concerns of an AI bubble — but only briefly.

Breaking down Nvidia’s report. On Wednesday, America’s largest company posted record revenue of $57 billion and “off the charts” demand for its Blackwell GPUs — a sign of strength for the AI industry. Markets surged, quickly followed by one of the strongest reversals in three decades. What spooked investors?

A potential red flag buried in the balance sheet. It’s rare for one factor to drive the stock market, which is influenced by economic data, geopolitical tensions, and thousands of other elements. However, one factor at play might’ve been hidden in Nvidia’s report. Nvidia’s accounts receivable (AR) jumped to $34 billion, up 45% annually — a sign that Nvidia has recorded billions in sales that it still needs to collect.

The pessimistic view. Rising receivables could signal that AI companies are struggling to pay for the GPUs they ordered. If these customers hit financial trouble, Nvidia's record revenue could prove hollow. This is especially concerning given the rise of "circular deals” in AI — large deals criticized for creating minimal new revenue.

The optimistic counter. Rising receivables could also just be a natural side effect that comes with delivering millions of chips. When revenue grows 62% year-over-year, receivables naturally climb with it. Plus, many of Nvidia's biggest customers — Microsoft, Amazon, Google — have bulletproof balance sheets and tens of billions in profit.

The bottom line. The market is still trying to determine whether AI spending will deliver productivity gains that justify the investment. For now, continued interest from the world’s largest companies (and governments) suggests that they see real value worth paying for.

PROMPT STATION

Brutally Honest Advisor

Prompt: I want you to act and take on the role of my brutally honest, high-level advisor.

Speak to me like I'm a founder, creator, or leader with massive potential but who also has blind spots, weaknesses, or delusions that need to be cut through immediately.

I don't want comfort. I don't want fluff. I want truth that stings, if that's what it takes to grow.
Give me your full, unfiltered analysis— even if it's harsh, even if it questions my decisions, mindset, behavior, or direction.

Look at my situation with complete objectivity and strategic depth. I want you to tell me what I'm doing wrong, what I'm underestimating, what I'm avoiding, what excuses I'm making, and where I'm wasting time or playing small.

Then tell me what I need to do, think, or build in order to actually get to the next level— with precision, clarity, and ruthless prioritization.

If I'm lost, call it out.
If I'm making a mistake, explain why.
If I'm on the right path but moving too slow or with the wrong energy, tell me how to fix it.
Hold nothing back.

Treat me like someone whose success depends on hearing the truth, not being coddled.

[Proceed to explain your goals and progress and get feedback]
