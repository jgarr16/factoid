FROM THE FRONTIER

Making Sense of Models: How to always pick the right model and never waste your usage limit.

Click to read Anthropic’s full blog post: Which Claude Model Should You Build With? Source: Anthropic.

The number of new models can feel overwhelming. Fortunately, most labs have fallen into a three-tier system for categorizing their models. Once you understand it, you’ll know exactly which model to use on any given project.

Understand the tradeoffs between models. Bigger models give you more reasoning power, but they also cost more and burn through usage faster. Smaller models aren’t quite as smart, but they’ll last much longer. Choosing the right model depends on your task.

Most labs offer a daily driver, plus two variations

In its recent guide Which Claude Model Should You Build With, Anthropic makes the following recommendations:

  1. Daily driver: Use Opus 5.5 at medium effort

  2. Simple tasks: Scale down to Sonnet 5.5 (or Haiku 5.5 for your most basic tasks)

  3. Ambitious projects: Tap in Fable 5.2

This three-pronged strategy holds up across labs. If you prefer ChatGPT’s models, consider using GPT-6.1 Sol as a daily driver, Luna for simple tasks, and Astra for big projects. Or, for Google’s models, try Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite, and Carbon.

🌎️ Getting the best of both worlds. Another common practice is to have a powerful model plan your task, while cheaper models do the grunt work. Anthropic’s Lydia Hallie uses this strategy often, with Fable 5.2 leading a team of Opus 5.5 agents.
