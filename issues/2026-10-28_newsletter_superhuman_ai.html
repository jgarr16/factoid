TODAY IN AI

Watch how Genie 4 generates interactive worlds in real time. Source: DeepMind.

1. Google reveals its most realistic world model yet: Genie 4 generates interactive simulations of 3D worlds in real time that include advanced physics, native audio, and real-time controls. After Genie creates a world, you can continue prompting the model to keep exploring and determine where the scene goes next. It’s coming soon to Google AI Ultra subscribers (currently waitlist-only). Google wasn’t the only lab to release a new world model: SpAItial AI’s Echo-3 builds 3D environments from phone scans of real rooms.

2. Anthropic claims Claude is speeding up drug discovery: Anthropic has been working to develop new medicines with six partner organizations, including Bristol Myers Squibb. The lab shared early results claiming Claude has advanced progress both in the lab and behind the scenes: helping Generate Biomedicines design a more effective cancer-therapy component, and cutting Enveda’s document review time by 89%. Notably, Claude’s contributions have mostly been in the early stages of drug development.

3. OpenAI announces ChatGPT plugins for Apple’s Mail and Calendar: The new update makes it easier to use ChatGPT across Apple’s ecosystem, letting you ask questions about your emails or create new events in your calendar. It’s available now on all ChatGPT plans. To get started, go to Settings > Plugins in ChatGPT, install iCloud Mail and iCloud Calendar, and sign in to each with your Apple app-specific password.

FROM THE FRONTIER

Apple reveals the iPad’s “biggest update ever” ahead of Pro and Tell, along with a new MacBook and iMac

Image Source: Apple.

Apple’s Pro and Tell event is going down today, but the tech giant couldn’t wait. It’s already unveiled three new devices.

The iPad mini gets its “biggest update ever,” featuring…

  • A brighter screen: The OLED display measures 8.5 inches, with peak brightness that’s twice as powerful as the old model, along with spill and dust resistance.

  • The A20 Pro chip: The new iPad serves AI features up to 4.5x faster than the last model.

  • Sound through the screen: An Acoustic Display sends bass from the woofers through the glass.

The 14-inch MacBook Pro delivers…

  • More power: The MacBook Pro loads graphics up to 30% faster than its predecessor, powered by the new M6 chip along with Apple’s latest operating system.

  • Stronger on-device AI: It processes LLM prompts up to 1.3x faster than the M5 and 13x faster than the M1 MacBook Pro.

  • Longer battery life: It can run for 24 hours on a full battery and charges from 0% to 50% in 30 minutes with a high-wattage adapter.

The new iMac is loaded with…

  • More AI power: It ships with the M6 and macOS 27, which includes Siri AI and Apple Intelligence. All that helps it serve on-device AI up to 5x faster than the M4.

  • A speed jump: Its graphics are up to 2x faster than the M4.

  • Sleek styles: The thin desktop comes in seven colors and has a 24-inch 4.5K Retina display.

Pre-orders for all three start today and ship_ _November 6th.
