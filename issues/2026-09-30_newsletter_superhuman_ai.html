🧸 OpenAI ships an "adorable" rival to Meta's Muse

Welcome back, Superhuman. AI labs realized they probably went too far with the doom and gloom messaging. Now they’re going the opposite direction, with personal assistants that are powerful and adorable. Move over, Muse. OpenAI just shipped dots.

Today: Manus 2.0 is here, learn how to recreate the viral AI rap memes with Higgsfield AI, and get the latest prompts and trending social posts.

TODAY IN AI

Watch how OpenAI’s new dots personal agents work. Source: OpenAI.

1. OpenAI ships “remarkably capable and cute” personal agents: OpenAI’s dots are always-on personal agents that can handle both work and personal tasks for you, all powered by GPT-6 Astra. Dots run on their own cloud computers, learn from feedback over time, and work with 4,000+ apps. Rolling out to Pro and Business users now. Explore ChatGPT dots, or keep scrolling to learn everything OpenAI announced at DevDay 2026.

2. Manus overhauls its chatbot to include video creation and Alchemy mode: Rebuilt on an entirely new architecture, Manus 2.0 expands beyond typical offerings like documents, slides, and websites into video and game creation. The update also features Cue, Manus’ personal agent, and Alchemy mode, which takes your rough ideas and turns them into finished videos. Explore Manus 2.0.

3. Meta introduces Muse for small business owners: Entrepreneurs can now loop in Muse to help run their business, whether that’s managing social pages, running operations, or finding new customers. Muse can take action across Meta’s apps (Instagram, Facebook, Meta Ads) as well as over a dozen connected tools like Asana, Canva, Intuit, Shopify, and more. Learn how it works.

TUTORIAL

How to recreate these viral AI rap memes with Higgsfield AI

Watch a viral AI rap video going viral on social media (4.5M views) Phone: @BasedDaedalus on X

  • Open Higgsfield AI, hover over ‘Video’, and select ‘Higgsfield Genjutsu’.

  • Upload a clip from the original video as the reference clip.

  • Add two character images showing the people or characters you want to appear in the new version. Assign each image to one of the two performers.

  • Add the following prompt:

Sample prompt: “Recreate this reference video using these two characters. Preserve the original camera angles, gestures, timing, facial expressions, pacing, and back-and-forth rap battle performance. Match the energy and body language of each performer closely, keep both characters visually consistent from shot to shot, and maintain realistic lip movement and facial animation”

  • Click ‘Generate’ and let Genjutsu recreate the clip using your uploaded characters.

  • Review the result for face consistency and character mix-ups.

  • Export your best version to TikTok or Reels. You can also try this with different clips from other music videos.

OpenAI subtly challenges two dominant productivity tools at its annual launch event

Made with Midjourney

OpenAI’s “dots” stole the show at DevDay 2026. But another new tool might actually be a bigger boost for your productivity, especially if you use Google Docs or Microsoft Word.

ChatGPT Space takes ChatGPT out of the chat window and into a shared work environment it calls Pages. Pages lets you create work documents and fine-tune them with human teammates or your preferred OpenAI tool. Collaborative slides and spreadsheets are coming soon. Here’s everything else OpenAI announced:

  • You can tag ChatGPT in Slack or Teams. Mention @ChatGPT in any thread or message and it’ll pull context from your conversation to help out.

* GPT-6.1 Sol nearly matches Astra’s performance at just one-fifth of its price.

  • ChatGPT subscriptions now work in 16 tools. A new “Sign in with ChatGPT” feature lets you access partner apps using your ChatGPT subscription.

But one product didn’t make the cut. OpenAI scrapped its latest model, GPT-6.1 Astra, after it showed “high levels” of deception during testing. Honestly…that’s fine with us.
