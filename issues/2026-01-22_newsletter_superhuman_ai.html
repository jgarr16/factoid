FROM THE FRONTIER

Your AI is an actor — and understanding its “character” is key to getting better results

Made with Midjourney

AI models are actors. Large Language Models (LLMs) absorb countless personas during training (heroes, villains, therapists, con artists), then get fine-tuned to play one role: the helpful Assistant. New research from Anthropic reveals how this character works — and how you can shape it to your advantage.

Assigning a character. Giving your AI model a professional character to play (consultant, analyst, editor, coach) anchors the model closer to its helpful "Assistant" persona — often leading to better results. If you feel like your model is offering sub-par answers, try reframing the conversation by having it adopt a professional persona.

This also mitigates risks. Anthropic’s research shows that the further away a chatbot gets from its default “assistant mode”, the more likely it is to comply with dangerous requests. Therapy and philosophical chats are especially risky. Anthropic uses a technique called activation capping to reduce this drift — but you can also protect yourself by keeping prompts grounded in professional archetypes.

Test it out for yourself. Anthropic partnered with Neuronpedia to let you see how AI models can drift off-course during a conversation. This demo lets you chat with a standard model vs. an activation-capped version — and watch persona drift happen in real-time.

THE AI ACADEMY

How predictive intelligence can streamline your sales pipeline at Salesforce

  1. In Salesforce, enable Einstein Lead Scoring from Setup.

  2. Go to settings and exclude irrelevant or noisy fields (like Zip Code or generic location data). This helps the model focus only on attributes that truly correlate with buying intent.

  3. Once the model is live, the Lead Scorecard displays a score from 1– 99. Crucially, the AI provides the "Top Positives" behind each score, giving reps the exact context they need for a personalized conversation.

  4. Go to Setup → Flow Builder and create a record-triggered flow on the Lead object.

Set the condition: Einstein Lead Score ≥ 85.

Automatically route these leads to a high-priority queue, notify reps, or create follow-up tasks instantly.

  1. By letting AI dictate the sequence, you remove the guesswork and focus energy where the intent is highest.
