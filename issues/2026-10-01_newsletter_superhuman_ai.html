Google surprises the industry with a new model: Gemini 4 Argon sets new highs on several benchmarks, with standout performance in coding, reasoning, and long-running tasks, along with an industry-leading million-token output. This pledge holds frontier labs responsible for safely developing AI, maintaining internal controls, and submitting to external audits when necessary. One catch: it doesn’t appear to be legally binding, but rather a voluntary promise.

How to recreate the viral “Bad Cat” style AI sitcoms with ChatGPT + Runway

Click here to check out a Bad-Cat sitcom clip — part of a series that’s going viral on social media. Photo:

  • Open ChatGPT > Images. Create a distinctive anthropomorphic pet with a consistent outfit, hairstyle, accessories, and personality.

  • Generate a recurring sitcom setting such as a slightly cluttered apartment, kitchen, balcony, or living room with a warm 90s-TV look.

Sample Prompt: “Create a recurring sitcom character: an anthropomorphic [animal] with [hair/style], wearing [outfit/accessories], living in a warm, slightly cluttered 1990s apartment with soft tungsten lighting, static multi-camera framing, subtle film grain, realistic textures, and expressive human-like body language. Create 6– 8 keyframes for a simple gag with a setup, escalation, reveal, and reaction.”

  • Upload each keyframe to Runway Image to Video and animate only the action needed for that specific shot.

  • Keep motion prompts simple, such as “the cat slowly opens the box,” “looks toward the doorway,” or “starts dancing confidently.”

  • Arrange the clips in order so the pacing builds toward one clear visual punchline.

  • Add a short title card, retro grading, light grain, and simple comedic audio before exporting for Reels or TikTok.

FROM THE FRONTIER

Revenue is soaring at OpenAI and Anthropic — but will that be enough to outrun their growing expenses?

Made with Midjourney

🤔 AI tools are undoubtedly popular, but are they profitable? This is a question investors have been trying to answer for months, and Anthropic’s leaked IPO prospectus just gave them some hard numbers to work with:

  • The good: Annual revenue hit $4.6B in 2025, up 12x from 2024.

  • The bad: It posted a $42B net loss during the same period.

  • The ugly: It’s on the hook for $518B in future cloud and compute commitments.

OpenAI is in a similar position: it lost $34B last year on $13B in revenue. But the situation is improving for both labs. And quickly. Anthropic’s annualized revenue reportedly hit $65B in July, while OpenAI’s has climbed to nearly $70B this year. Those are huge jumps, but there’s still one major unanswered question: Are expenses rising just as quickly? The general public won’t know until one of these labs IPOs, and its full financials documents are made public.

Zooming out: Consulting firm Bain & Co. estimates the AI industry is on track to spend nearly $6 trillion on new compute capacity by 2030. Existing demand will likely contribute $1.8T to that figure. That leaves a $4.2T revenue shortfall the industry needs to find for its immense spending to be justified.
