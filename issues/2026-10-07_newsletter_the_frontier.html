Google goes nuclear

Happy Tuesday, legends. Welcome back to another edition of The Frontier, our weekly newsletter covering the best new AI launches on Product Hunt.

WHAT'S HOT

⚡ Google is buying 3,590MW of power from Constellation Energy. 890MW of it comes from upgrading eleven existing reactors in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, so no new reactor gets built, the old ones just get better equipment and give up more. More than $4.3 billion of investment, and the first upgraded plant delivers in 2028. (Data Center Dynamics)

🧠 Mistral previewed Large 4, a trillion-parameter model it has nicknamed Le Chonk. 49 billion parameters active per token, a million-token context window, natively multimodal, trained end to end on 3,800 Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own European data centres. Weights land at the end of October. (MarkTechPost)

🔐 Meta and Sierra published a standard for how your agent logs into a shop. The Personal Agent Protocol is OAuth with tiers: an agent browses as a guest to check stock or a returns policy, and once you sign in you decide whether it gets read-only or write access. Walmart, Shopify, Stripe, Rocket, Genesys and Instinct are founding partners, with a v0.1 spec due this month. Shopify and Stripe have also joined Visa's rival protocol. (TNW)

⚖️ The Eighth Circuit paused Minnesota's nudification ban after xAI challenged it. The law took effect on 1 August and bars software from generating realistic intimate images of an identifiable person. xAI argues it restricts protected speech, the district court refused relief twice, and the appeals court granted it in a one-sentence order with no reasoning given. (Minnesota Lawyer)

🤖 Anthropic widened its Cyber Verification Program into three tiers. Project Glasswing has been folded in, and the tiers cover security professionals and incident response teams who need access the general consumer terms do not allow. (Anthropic)
