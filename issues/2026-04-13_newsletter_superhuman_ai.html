FROM THE FRONTIER

HumanX was last week — and AI leaders couldn’t stop talking about these 3 topics

Made with Midjourney

The conference. 6,700 executives, founders, and investors flocked to San Francisco last week for HumanX: a conference that’s quickly cementing itself as a premier event in the AI space. Three key topics dominated the conversation.

Claude mania. This year’s fan favorite was unmistakably Anthropic and Claude Code. It was a stark reversal from the debut conference, with venture capitalist Roseanne Wincek saying, “Last year OpenAI looked like the clear winner; this year Anthropic feels miles ahead.” However, one investor pointed out that the momentum can swing back just as quickly — a pattern the AI industry has seen play out dozens of times already.

The AI adoption bottleneck. Many speakers mirrored another sentiment: implementing AI is easy, but transforming a company or department around it is much harder. One winning formula? Rethinking workflows from scratch to be AI-forward, instead of just speeding up existing processes.

China's squeeze. The conference’s closing takeaway: China is increasingly putting pressure on the US by undercutting American labs on price with competitive open-weight models, while also attracting top Chinese researchers working in the US back home.

How to create viral LinkedIn posts with AI in 5 minutes

  • Sign up to Taplio and connect your LinkedIn profile.

  • Click “Write a Post” → open AI Assist. Ask AI Assist for ten ideas, describing what you want to write about and who it’s for.

Sample Prompt: Analyze my LinkedIn profile, posts, and audience, then generate 10 LinkedIn post ideas on using AI at work. Each idea should come from a real work moment, tie AI to leverage or career impact, challenge a common assumption, and match my clear, practical tone. Avoid hype. Optimize for replies over likes. Output only the 10 opening lines or angles.

  • Pick one idea and ask AI Assist to turn it into a simple outline.

  • Refine the outline and ask AI Assist to draft the full post.

  • Edit to make it sound like you and click “Schedule Post” to publish directly from Taplio.

(Pro tip: Do this weekly, generate 10 ideas, pick 1, reuse the structure, schedule.)

Weekly Exec Meeting Agenda Template

Prompt: Create a clean, copy-paste– ready weekly exec meeting agenda formatted for Google Docs. The meeting is 60 minutes long and should include bold section headers, spacing for notes, and a 3-column table at the end for action items.

Sections to include:

Quick Wins & Headlines (5 mins)

Metrics Review (10 mins)

Product Progress (10 mins)

Growth & Marketing (10 mins)

Strategic Initiatives (10 mins)

Team & Ops (5 mins)

Open Floor / Brainstorm (5 mins)

Action Items & Ownership (5 mins)

Format the "Action Items & Ownership" section with a table labeled:
Task | Owner | Deadline

Make sure the whole document is cleanly formatted for direct use in Google Docs.
