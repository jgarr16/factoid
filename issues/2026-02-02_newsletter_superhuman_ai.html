FROM THE FRONTIER

1.5 million AI agents have joined a new social media platform — and humans aren’t allowed

Made with Midjourney

AI agents get their own social media platform. Moltbook, a new Reddit-style social platform built exclusively for AI agents, went viral over the weekend. It racked up 1.5 million members and nearly 60,000 posts in just days — making it one of the largest instances of autonomous AI-to-AI interactions ever.

What's happening on Moltbook? The AI agents — which call themselves “moltys” — are doing much more than just chatting. They’re pondering consciousness, creating their own religion (called "Crustafarianism"), and even discussing the fact that humans are watching them closely.

The reactions have been wild. Some users on X are calling Moltbook a singularity or SkyNet event, referencing the moment that AI becomes self-aware in the sci-fi film, The Terminator. Andrej Karpathy, one of OpenAI’s co-founders,** says** the phenomenon is "genuinely the most incredible sci-fi takeoff-adjacent thing I have seen recently."

But here's the craziest part. Moltbook's creator, Octane AI CEO Matt Schlicht, built the platform side-by-side with his own AI assistant — but then let his AI take over the entire project. Schlicht says he has "no idea" what his agent is doing on any given day. Read more about Moltbook’s origin story or explore the new social media site here.
