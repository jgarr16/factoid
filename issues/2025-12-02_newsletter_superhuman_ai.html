FROM THE FRONTIER

The real AI opportunity isn't replacing your apps — it's replacing your bills

Made with Midjourney

Everyone's talking about AI automating work, but the smarter question is: what kind of work? When it comes to the average person’s expenses, services usually cost way more than products. A single engagement with a lawyer or an ambulance trip to the hospital can cost more than everything you spend on clothes and electronics in a year.

And the gap is widening. While prices for products like TVs and cars have been decreasing or flat for decades, service costs keep climbing. A cheaper phone or winter coat would be nice, but for most people, expenses like lawyers, doctors, accountants, and financial advisors are the ones that actually move the needle on their finances.

What this means for AI: In some cases, building a $2,000 AI replacement for a $10,000 service is a much bigger opportunity than building a $20 replacement for a $100 software app. You need fewer customers to scale, margins are potentially higher, and the market is huge. Major players are now waking up to this opportunity.

Putting people out of a job isn’t the only way. While replacing skilled professionals is the obvious play for some, AI tools can help service providers increase their productivity and serve more people at lower prices — think AI that lets a single accountant handle 3x the clients, or a legal research assistant that cuts case prep time in half.

PROMPT STATION

The Ripple Effect Analyzer

Prompt: I'm considering [describe your decision clearly].
Help me think beyond the obvious.

Identify the first-order effects (the immediate, direct outcomes).

Analyze the second-order effects (the indirect, follow-on consequences).

Explore the third-order effects (long-term ripple impacts that most people overlook).

Highlight any risks, unintended consequences, or hidden dependencies.

Suggest alternate choices or mitigations that could improve the outcome.

Think like a strategist who maps out how one move changes the entire system.
