FROM THE FRONTIER

Big Tech’s earnings reports sent a clear message to investors

Made with Midjourney

Big Tech’s monster week. Big Tech collectively crushed its earnings reports last week. Apple, Meta, and Microsoft all beat investor expectations, with Apple and Meta posting record quarterly revenues of $143.8B and $59.9B, respectively. But investors are still concerned about one thing.

Revenue is climbing, but so are expenses. The race to “own” the future of AI is still accelerating. And quickly. Meta and Microsoft alone plan to spend a combined quarter trillion dollars on AI infrastructure, and they’re not alone. Last year’s capital expenditures were already significant — and the bill is on-track to keep growing in 2026.

The message to investors. Big Tech’s continued spending sends a clear message: AI is the future, and investment now will determine who dominates later. The playbook itself is nothing new, as it’s common for tech companies to burn millions, or even billions, while they scale. But hundreds of billions? This is uncharted territory.

The elephant in the room. Consumer demand for AI is high — but its profitability? Still murky. With hundreds of billions on the line, AI will need to generate serious revenue to justify the investment. Until it does, expect the tug-of-war between record spending and unproven returns to continue defining earnings calls.

THE AI ACADEMY

How to record and summarize meetings with ChatGPT

  • Download the ChatGPT desktop app and sign in with your account.

  • Look for the record button at the bottom of any chat. It's next to where you'd normally type.

  • Click it and deal with permissions. Your Mac will ask for microphone access, then system audio access.

  • Start talking. ChatGPT live-transcribes as you talk. You can pause/resume freely.

  • Hit "Stop" when done.

  • Choose "Upload" to send the transcript to the ChatGPT server for processing. Within seconds, it opens a private canvas with a structured summary.

  • Ask anything you like about your meeting.

_Sample Prompt: _“Highlight any risks, blockers, or unresolved questions”

Please make sure you have everyone’s permission in the meeting to record.
