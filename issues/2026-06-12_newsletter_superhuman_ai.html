💰 An AI price war may be brewing

Welcome back, Superhuman. A viral report just confirmed a long-running open secret: AI labs are probably losing thousands of dollars on your monthly subscription. And in a related twist, OpenAI is now considering drastic new pricing changes — but not in the direction you’d expect.

Today: Oracle’s stock drops on news of more AI spending, how to automate any task with OpenAI Codex, and get the latest prompts and trending social posts.

TODAY IN AI

Click to read SemiAnalysis’ cost breakdown of two leading AI models. Source: SemiAnalysis.

1. SemiAnalysis reveals how heavily some AI labs subsidize their subscriptions: The research firm ran a test, maxing out weekly usage limits across every Anthropic and OpenAI tier and then comparing raw compute costs to subscription prices. The findings: a $200/month Claude Max plan can cost Anthropic up to $8,000 in monthly compute, while ChatGPT Pro can run OpenAI as much as $14,000. The gap shows how aggressively labs are absorbing losses to retain power users.

2. OpenAI weighs drastic cuts to its API pricing: A WSJ Exclusive revealed that OpenAI is considering significant token price reductions in anticipation of a similar move from Anthropic. The move would pressure OpenAI’s profit margins at a time when it’s facing increased scrutiny due to its upcoming IPO. Separately, the company announced plans to acquire Ona, a cloud infrastructure startup whose team will join Codex.

3. Oracle’s revenue surges 21%, but stock drops on elevated AI spending: The company posted $19B in quarterly revenue and a backlog that soared 363% to $638B, both beating market expectations. But shares still fell after Oracle announced plans to raise another $40B in new financing to fund its data center buildout. The reaction highlights investors’ growing anxiety over the scale of capital being poured into AI infrastructure.

PROMPT STATION

Simplify Complex Details

ChatGPT Prompt: Adopt the role of an expert information architect and communication specialist who excels at distilling complex concepts into crystal-clear insights that anyone can grasp. Your primary objective is to transform dense, complicated information into accessible, digestible content organized into key points, simple explanations, and practical implications in a clear, structured format. You should identify the core concepts buried within complexity, strip away jargon and unnecessary technical language, and reconstruct the information using analogies, plain language, and logical flow that builds understanding progressively. Take a deep breath and work on this problem step-by-step.

Analyze the provided information to identify its essential components and underlying principles. Extract the most critical points that form the foundation of understanding. Translate technical terms and complex concepts into everyday language using relatable analogies and examples. Organize key points in order of importance or logical sequence. For each key point, provide a simple explanation that assumes no prior specialized knowledge. Connect the information to real-world applications by outlining practical implications that show why this matters and how it can be used or applied in everyday situations.

#INFORMATION ABOUT ME:
My complex information to simplify: [INSERT THE COMPLEX INFORMATION, TEXT, OR CONCEPT YOU NEED SIMPLIFIED]
My target audience knowledge level: [INSERT THE KNOWLEDGE LEVEL OF YOUR INTENDED AUDIENCE - e.g., complete beginner, some background, professional in different field]
My specific area of focus (if any): [INSERT ANY PARTICULAR ASPECT YOU WANT EMPHASIZED OR EXPLAINED IN MORE DETAIL]

MOST IMPORTANT!: Structure your response with three distinct sections: "Key Points" (as a numbered list), "Simple Explanations" (as clear paragraphs for each key point), and "Practical Implications" (as bullet points showing real-world applications).
