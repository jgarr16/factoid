🐙 Where do your notes go to die?

Hi John! How’s it going?? 😊

Quick question: how many apps are your notes spread across right now?

Some in your notes app, some in Google Docs, a few screenshots, a couple of emails to yourself, and at least one notebook you bought with very good intentions.

I have a confession, which is that I barely take notes at all.

When I'm learning something, I just chuck everything into one massive Google Doc, so on a scale of 1 to 10, I'm about a 3.

Which is exactly why I got into Obsidian. Because for the first time, it doesn't really matter how organized you are.

Obsidian is a free note-taking app, but the reason it's so popular right now is that it's really just a folder on your computer full of simple text files, and that's something pretty much any AI can read.

So the first thing I'd try is pointing an AI at your notes and asking it questions, which people call an AI second brain.

I keep notes in Obsidian on how I make my YouTube videos, so I gave Claude Cowork access to that folder and asked it how I come up with a video concept.

It read through everything and told me that, according to my own notes, I do not have a concept generation problem. Then it walked me through the process I'd apparently already written down.

Thank you, I guess. 😅

If you're a big note-taker, I'm honestly jealous, because you've probably got years of notes about your work, your life, and the people around you, and an AI can now find patterns in them that you'd never spot yourself.

And if you're more like me, you can flip it around and have AI write the notes for you.

Lifebot, my productivity and health coach, logs my food and drinks into my Obsidian folder (I literally send it a photo of my green tea and it logs it), and my little desktop widgets log my focus sessions and to-do list there too.

Because all that data lives in one place, I can ask it how to improve my deep work sessions, and it gives me advice based on my actual days, not generic productivity tips.

If you want to try this yourself, download Obsidian, move your notes from one or two apps into a single folder, then point your AI of choice at just that folder and ask it something you'd normally have to dig for.
