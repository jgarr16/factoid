FROM THE FRONTIER

AI companies want chatbots to feel personal, but Americans are skeptical

Made with Midjourney

AI chatbots want to take the relationship to the next level. Elon Musk’s Grok lets users create personal animated avatars for conversations, while Perplexity offers personalized AI companions. In a recent post, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman also indicated that ChatGPT will soon permit mature and erotic content. But a new Pew Research survey finds half of Americans are more worried than excited about AI’s growing presence in their lives.

So what do people want? According to the Pew survey, people are fine with AI handling tasks like weather forecasts or fraud detection, but they want it to stay clear of personal matters. Nearly two-thirds of Americans want AI out of their love lives entirely, and 73% say it should never advise on religion. Other concerns include AI weakening creativity, human connection, and critical thinking.

Even digital natives aren’t thrilled. Young Americans, the ones most exposed to AI, are also the most skeptical. Fifty-seven percent of people under 30 fear it’s damaging their thinking skills, compared to 46% of seniors. Usage patterns mirror this caution. The AP-NORC poll shows only about 4 in 10 use AI for work, one-third for emails or entertainment, and just one-quarter for shopping.

Policymakers echo public concerns. A Stanford study shows that regulators are strongly backing rules on AI, particularly around data privacy (80%) and retraining displaced workers (76%). Attitudes towards AI may soften over time. But for now, it’s the coworker that’s trying a little too hard to be your friend.

PROMPT STATION

Go-To-Market Plan for B2B SaaS

Prompt: You are a COO/CFO-level operator and strategy consultant.
Objective: Produce a **Go-To-Market Plan** for a** B2B SaaS** company that prioritizes clarity, numbers, and decision-readiness.

Inputs you will receive (paste or attach as bullet points or a table):
- Business model (e.g., PLG, enterprise sales, marketplace), ICP, pricing tiers
- Current ARR/MRR, growth rate, ARPA/ARPU, gross margin, sales cycle
- CAC (by channel), payback period, churn (logo & net), LTV, NRR/GRR
- Pipeline by stage, top 5 risks, constraints (headcount/budget/tech/compliance)

If any inputs are missing, ask **exactly 3 clarification questions**, then proceed with reasoned assumptions (state them explicitly).
