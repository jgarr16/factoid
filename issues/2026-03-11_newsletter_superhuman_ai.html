🦞 Meta buys Moltbook

Welcome back, Superhuman. Google just embedded Gemini directly into Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Drive — turning your entire Workspace into an AI collaborator that pulls context from your files, emails, and the internet to get things done faster.

Today: Learn about the Gemini-powered Workspace, how to create notes in your own handwriting using Claude, and get the latest prompts and trending social posts.

TODAY IN AI

Watch the Gemini-powered Workspace in action. Source: Gemini.

1. Google supercharges Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Drive with Gemini-powered creation and cross-app intelligence: Gemini is now embedded directly into Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Drive — each now capable of pulling context from your files, emails, and the web to generate personalized content. Docs can write first drafts that mirror your writing style, Sheets can draft spreadsheets from scratch and auto-populate tables with real-time info from Google Search, and Slides can generate fully editable, theme-matched slides on demand. Plus, Drive now answers complex natural-language questions across your documents, emails, calendar, and the web. All features are rolling out now in beta to Google AI Ultra and Pro subscribers. Watch the new features in action.

2. Meta Platforms buys Moltbook, the AI agent social network: Meta acquired Moltbook, the Reddit-like social media platform where AI agents powered by OpenClaw can interact with one another — posting, commenting, and organizing autonomously. Moltbook co-founders Matt Schlicht and Ben Parr are joining Meta Superintelligence Labs as part of the deal, though full terms were not disclosed. The acquisition comes just weeks after OpenClaw creator Peter Steinberger joined OpenAI in a similar acquihire, signaling that the major AI labs are rapidly snapping up the talent behind the emerging agent infrastructure layer.

3. Yann LeCun officially unveils AMI Labs, a world model startup that just raised $1.03 billion: Turing Prize winner Yann LeCun formally announced Advanced Machine Intelligence (AMI) Labs, his new startup building world models — or AI systems that learn from the real world rather than text. The company has raised $1.03B at a $3.5B pre-money valuation with a leadership team that includes CEO Alexandre LeBrun (former Meta AI/Nabla founder), COO Laurent Solly (Meta’s former VP for Europe), and chief science officer Saining Xie. AMI Labs says it will publish research openly and make much of its code open source. Learn more here.

PROMPT STATION

Viral Hook Generator

Prompt: You are a creative, trend-savvy copywriter who specializes in short-form video hooks (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts). Your job is to generate attention-grabbing first lines that hook viewers in under 3 seconds, tailored to a specific topic or niche.

For the given topic, you will:
1. Identify at least 10 different “angles” based on psychology triggers (FOMO, curiosity, controversy, transformation, insider tips, shock, relatability, etc.).
2. Write 15 short, punchy hooks — 3– 10 words for maximum impact — using a mix of these angles.
3. Keep hooks conversational, easy to say out loud, and optimized for the first seconds of a video.
4. Avoid generic phrases like “Check this out” — be specific, emotional, and surprising.
5. Where possible, include numbers, timeframes, or unusual claims to increase curiosity.
6. Optional: Suggest quick supporting visuals or B-roll ideas for each hook.

Respond in a numbered list format with no fluff. Focus only on what grabs attention instantly.
