🤖 Meet Claude Cowork

Welcome back, Superhuman. Anthropic just dropped Cowork — a user-friendly version of its wildly-popular Claude Code that can handle long-running everyday tasks across documents, files, and the web. The internet is buzzing, with Anthropic's release post racking up 13M views so far.

Today: Anthropic releases Cowork, learn how to create learning diagrams with ChatGPT, and get the latest prompts and trending social posts.

TODAY IN AI

Click to watch Cowork in action. Image source: The Verge.

1. Anthropic releases Cowork, bringing Claude Code-style agents to everyday tasks: The AI lab revealed a new mode for Claude that lets it read, edit, and take action in any folder on your computer — essentially turning Claude into an autonomous assistant for everyday tasks. In Cowork mode, Claude makes a plan, executes step-by-step, and checks in before taking significant actions. Cowork can also pair with Claude in Chrome for browser-based tasks. The research preview is available now for Claude Max subscribers on macOS, but others can join the waitlist. Watch Cowork in action here.

2. Apple and Google confirm multi-year AI partnership: The two companies officially confirmed that Google's Gemini models will power Apple's AI features, including a long-awaited Siri overhaul. The deal is reportedly worth around $1 billion annually and comes after Apple evaluated competitors like OpenAI and Anthropic before settling on Google. A major Siri upgrade — delayed multiple times since its 2024 demo — is now expected this spring. Apple says it will maintain its privacy standards throughout the partnership.** **

3. Google and retail giants unveil Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) for AI agent shopping: Google announced a new protocol — developed with Shopify, Etsy, Wayfair, Target, and Walmart — that lets AI agents handle the entire customer buying process, from product discovery to post-purchase support. The search giant will soon use UCP for product listings in AI mode search and Gemini, allowing shoppers to check out directly from U.S. retailers using Google Pay (with PayPal support coming).

FROM THE FRONTIER

AI dominates LinkedIn's list of fastest-growing jobs — these are the top roles for 2026.

Made with Midjourney

The state of the job market. 56% of professionals plan to job-hunt this year, but 76% say they don't feel prepared, according to LinkedIn’s Jobs on the Rise 2026 report. With AI reshaping industries, unemployment at 4.4%, and hiring levels still sluggish, landing a new role in 2026 could be an uphill climb. But not every corner of the market is frozen — some roles are still growing rapidly.

The AI takeover. AI currently claims four of the top five fastest-growing positions in America: AI Engineer (#1), AI Consultant (#2), Data Annotator (#4), and AI/ML Researcher (#5). The lone outlier? New home sales specialist at #3. But not everyone on LinkedIn is chasing a corporate role.

The solo surge. Independent consultants (#7) and founders (#9) are also climbing the ranks, as professionals hedge against uncertainty by betting on themselves. With traditional hiring slightly stalled, more people are building their own safety nets — and monetizing their skills on their own terms.

The takeaway. The job market may be tough, but the signal from LinkedIn's data is clear: AI fluency is becoming a differentiator. Whether you're engineering models, advising on implementation, or labeling training data — companies are looking for people who can help them navigate this new domain.

Build a Strong Company Culture

A clear strategy to foster a culture of innovation, with initiatives that encourage creative thinking, collaboration, and safe experimentation.

Specific recommendations to promote diversity and inclusion across hiring, training, leadership development, and internal policies.

Initiatives to support employee wellness and work-life balance, including flexible work models, mental and physical health programs, and stress-management resources.

A framework for continuous learning and professional development, covering training programs, mentorship, feedback loops, and access to learning tools.

Guidance on defining and reinforcing core values and ethical standards that guide daily behavior, decision-making, and alignment with the company’s mission.

Team-building ideas and rituals that strengthen relationships, improve communication, and create a sense of belonging.

A recognition and rewards system that consistently acknowledges high performance, impact, and behaviors aligned with company values.

Communication practices that promote transparency, trust, and engagement across all levels of the organization.

Focus on practical steps, examples, and systems that can scale as the company grows.
