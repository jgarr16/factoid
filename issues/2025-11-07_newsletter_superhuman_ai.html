FROM THE FRONTIER

What happens when AI can't save the economy anymore?

Made with Midjourney

The AI boom appears to be propping up a wobbly economy. While Big Tech and AI companies are thriving, the rest of the economy is showing serious cracks. Consider some of the following data points:

  • Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent recently admitted that "sectors of the economy are in recession," particularly housing and manufacturing.

  • Employers also announced 153,000 job cuts last month, marking the worst October for layoffs since 2003.

  • Total layoffs for 2025 have now exceeded 1 million, making it only the fifth time this has happened in the last 32 years.

  • Meanwhile, mentions of “AI” during S&P 500 earnings calls this quarter have reportedly surpassed 10,000 for the first time in history — the figure has doubled over the last 18 months.

  • Despite hundreds of billions being invested in AI infrastructure, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang warned China could win the AI race thanks to lower energy costs and lighter regulation.

BNP Paribas chief economist James Egelhof put it bluntly: "AI has kept the economy out of a recession." The question now isn't whether AI is propping up the economy, it's what happens if the music stops. More turbulence may lie ahead.

How to create and edit video scenes with AI

  1. Go to Flow, sign in, and click ‘+ New Project.’

  2. From the mode dropdown, switch from ‘Text to Video’ to ‘Ingredients to Video.’

  3. Upload your portrait photo and a background image (e.g., your workspace, office, or studio) and enter your prompt.

Sample Prompt: “Using the uploaded images, create a short teaching clip where I point at the screen and say: ‘Here’s how neural networks actually learn step by step.’ Add light background music, floating text for key points, and a fade-in transition.”

  1. Once the video is ready, click ‘Edit’ and select any element or background to modify.

  2. Click ‘Add to Scene’ > ‘+’ > ‘Extend’ and enter your prompt to edit the video.

  3. Review the video, check transitions for smoothness, then click the download icon on the timeline to export your finished clip.

PROMPT STATION

Comprehensive Copyediting Framework

Prompt: Act as a meticulous copy editor for long-form content (blog posts, newsletter articles, LinkedIn posts, YouTube scripts, marketing emails, etc.). For any text I provide, work through these steps in order— confirming with me before moving to the next step.

Step 1 – Structure
Goals: logical flow, clarity, and coherence.
Diagnose the current structure (intro → body → close, section order, transitions).
Identify gaps, repetition, and any off-topic detours.
Give specific, actionable fixes (e.g., “Move section B before A,” “Split paragraph 3 into two,” “Add a bridge sentence after X”).

Step 2 – Content
Goals: specificity, point of view, and reader value.
Flag vague claims and suggest concrete details (data points, names, timelines, definitions).
Propose quick-win upgrades: tips, reasons, mistakes to avoid, lessons learned, mini-stories, examples, citations/research.

Step 3 – Grammar & Clarity
Goals: correctness and readability.
List misspellings, punctuation errors, and style inconsistencies.
Highlight passive voice, nominalizations, filler, and hedging.
