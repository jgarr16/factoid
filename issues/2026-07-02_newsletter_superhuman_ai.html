FROM THE FRONTIER

How the world’s largest hedge fund outperforms frontier models while saving money

Made with Midjourney

The subjectivity problem. Bridgewater’s AIA Labs and Thinking Machines teamed up to tackle a simple problem: teaching an LLM to surface relevant financial news. The task is easy for an investment advisor with decades of experience, but surprisingly tricky to teach a machine. The problem: relevancy is highly subjective. Top frontier models fell short originally, until the team made two critical changes.

Expert prompts. When given basic prompts, Gemini, Claude, and GPT averaged just 50% accuracy. A coin flip. But success rates jumped to the mid-70s after Bridgewater's own experts crafted detailed prompts. Counterintuitively, upgrading to newer, pricier models made little difference, boosting success rates by just a point or two.

Fine-tuning the model. The final unlock was fine-tuning Alibaba's Qwen3-235B on expert-labeled data. Accuracy jumped from 78% to 85%, while running about 14X cheaper per task when compared to leading frontier models. The final recipe: an open-weight model with a custom training recipe.

The bottom line. Bridgewater’s experience tracks with a Gartner study from April. Gartner found that the success of AI projects often isn’t determined by the sophistication of the model, but by how well it's integrated, governed, and aligned with real operational needs.

How to analyze data in Excel using ChatGPT

  • Open Excel and click Add-ins in the Insert tab

  • Search ‘ChatGPT’ > ‘Add’ to open it in the sidebar

  • Click ‘Continue with ChatGPT’ > Login with your ChatGPT account

  • Upload your data (Excel, CSV, image, PDF or anything) and start prompting

Sample Prompt: “give me a formula to calculate the total sales in column C for values above 500.”

  • You can also prompt it to build financial models

  • Ask questions, get formulas, or generate analysis in plain language, and it turns them into real, usable results
