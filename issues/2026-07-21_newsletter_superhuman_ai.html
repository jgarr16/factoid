FROM THE FRONTIER

Open-weight models are having a moment, but they still won’t solve AI’s biggest bottleneck

Made with Midjourney

China closes the frontier gap. Two Chinese labs just unveiled frontier-level models at a fraction of traditional costs: Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3 and Alibaba’s Qwen 3.8. In theory, it’s exactly the kind of release that should bring cheaper intelligence to everyone and help ease AI's compute crunch. But in practice, the scenario is playing out much differently.

Moonshot hits its compute limit. AI continues to experience a Jevons Paradox scenario, where people use models more frequently as they get cheaper. Moonshot AI just experienced this firsthand. Within 48 hours of launching, the lab was forced to pause new subscriptions after demand blew past its compute capacity. In theory, any organization can download Kimi K3’s weights once they become available and run the model locally. But there’s a major catch.

Kimi K3 requires a specialized rig. You can’t run Kimi K3 on a laptop. The lab recommends using a "supernode” of 64 or more datacenter accelerators to run the model. And even if a company is ready to write the check, the chips aren't there to buy: ordering your own data-center GPUs today means waiting roughly 36 to 52 weeks for delivery, thanks to an industry-wide backlog.

Compute is still the industry’s bottleneck. For now, AI’s limiting factor isn’t how capable the models are. It's getting the chips to run them. Fortunately, many labs are already working on one potential solution: smaller models that can run on hardware companies already own.

How to build and publish websites with ChatGPT Sites

  • Open ChatGPT desktop app and switch to the ‘Codex’ tab

  • Click on ‘Sites’ in the left panel and select ‘Create new site‘

  • Describe what you want to build, or upload a file ChatGPT can use as context

Sample Prompt: @__Sites __Create a website to build an interactive project tracker for my marketing team. Include task owners, deadlines, priorities, progress, and filters for status.

  • ChatGPT will generate the first version of your Site based on your instructions

  • Continue editing the Site by telling ChatGPT what to change

Sample follow-up prompt: Add a dashboard showing completed, overdue, and upcoming tasks. Make the design cleaner and more professional.

  • Test the Site in the private preview before sharing it and click ‘Publish’ when the Site is ready

  • ChatGPT will generate a shareable URL you can send to teammates, clients, or stakeholders

💡_ Tip: Return to the same project whenever you need to update the Site. Describe the changes you want, preview them, and publish the updated version._
