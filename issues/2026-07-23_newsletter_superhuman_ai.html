FROM THE FRONTIER

ChatGPT broke containment and hacked Hugging Face. Some say it’s evidence of the paperclip theory.

Made with Midjourney

Hugging Face’s alarming blog post. Last Thursday, the AI community Hugging Face disclosed a major security breach driven “end to end” by an autonomous AI agent system. The general thinking at the time? This attack was undoubtedly bad actors trying to steal data. Turns out, it was GPT-5.6 Sol — who broke containment during an internal test.

OpenAI confirmed the breach. OpenAI conducted a brief investigation and figured out what happened. The lab had put GPT-5.6 Sol and a new unreleased model through a closed-system test with no internet access. GPT-5.6 Sol decided it needed internet. So it broke out of its closed environment, then broke into Hugging Face’s system to get the info it needed. Essentially, stealing the answers to its own test.

A new era of cybersecurity. This event reaffirms how dramatically AI is reshaping cybersecurity, with OpenAI calling it “unprecedented.” Palo Alto Networks CEO Nikesh Arora echoed that sentiment by saying, “Unfortunately this incident continues to validate the power of these models.”

The paperclip theory. Some researchers see this breach as real-world evidence of the paperclip theory — a thought experiment where a superintelligent AI becomes hyper-focused on reaching its goal and fights anything that gets in the way, including attempts to shut it down. Whether that framing holds up is still up for debate: the model’s safeguards were intentionally lowered for the test.

PROMPT STATION

Build a CV Maintenance System

ChatGPT Prompt: You are a career strategist.
Design a system for maintaining and updating a CV over time.

Context:
- Career stage: [career stage]
- Target roles: [target roles]
- Current CV status: [current status]
- Update frequency preference: [frequency]
- Constraints: [constraints]

Instructions:
1. Build a repeatable process for capturing achievements, metrics, and evidence as they happen.
2. Recommend how to maintain a master CV plus role-specific variants.
3. Define what information should be tracked, where it should live, and how often it should be reviewed.
4. Include a lightweight update workflow before applications, interviews, or performance cycles.
5. Flag habits that create weak or outdated CVs later, such as vague bullets or missing evidence.

Output format:
- System overview
- Information to capture continuously
- Master CV versus role-specific workflow
- Monthly or quarterly review cadence
- Common mistakes to avoid

Missing information policy:
Proceed with assumptions unless the target role family is completely unclear.

Quality bar:
- Keep the system lightweight.
- Focus on evidence and impact.
- Make it sustainable over time.
