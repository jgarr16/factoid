FROM THE FRONTIER

Bit Tech has $1.65T in “hidden debt” that doesn’t show up on its balance sheet — and investors are getting nervous

Made with Midjourney

Big Tech’s debt pile. Borrowing money to fund growth is a routine part of corporate finance. But Big Tech has taken it to another level. Companies like Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Oracle have a massive debt pile coming due — and the US economy is riding on their ability to pay it.

Keeping it off the books. Big Tech has roughly $1.35T in reported debt on the books. But a new report from Nikkei Asia found that they also have $1.65T in “hidden debt” — or financial obligations not included on balance sheets. This primarily includes GPU contracts and data center leases, which aren’t accounted for until those facilities are up and running.

The big concern. Some investors are worried that Big Tech is more leveraged than their financial statements make it seem. As data centers come online, that hidden debt will start to come due. If AI demand doesn’t keep climbing, these projects could end up being much more expensive than they’re worth.

Holding up the economy. The Atlantic estimates that AI-linked firms have added roughly $27T in value over the past three years, equal to about 36% of the entire US stock market today, meaning a Big Tech stock selloff would have ripple effects throughout the economy. An update is coming soon: most of Big Tech reports earnings over the next two weeks.

How to turn your research into an interactive app

  • Go to Claude and turn on ‘Artifacts’ from Settings → Capabilities

  • Upload your research files, notes, PDFs, etc. or paste your raw research directly into the chat and enter the prompt:

“Summarize this research into the most important insights, patterns, data points, and user takeaways.”

  • Once research is complete, ask Claude to turn it into an interactive app:

“Create an interactive app from this research. Include a clean UI, filters, clickable sections, charts where useful, short explanations, and a simple user flow. Build it as a Claude Artifact.”

PROMPT STATION

Design an Accountability System

ChatGPT Prompt: You are designing an accountability system for sustained follow-through.
Use the context below.
Context:
- Goal type: [goal type]
- Personality or work style: [style]
- Current follow-through issues: [issues]
- Tools already used: [tools]
- Preferred review cadence: [cadence]
Instructions:
1. Diagnose why the current accountability approach is failing.
2. Recommend a simple system the person is likely to keep using.
3. Include check-ins, commitments, review triggers, and consequence or recovery mechanisms.
4. Reduce unnecessary friction and tracking overhead.
5. Explain how the system should evolve if consistency improves or drops.
Output format:
- Main accountability failure points
- Recommended system
- Weekly check-in format
- Recovery plan for missed commitments
Missing information policy:
Proceed with assumptions unless the goal type or main failure pattern is unclear.
Quality bar:
- Lightweight enough to use.
- Strong enough to matter.
- Not built on guilt alone.
