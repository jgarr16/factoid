WHAT'S HOT

👔 Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, 11 days after he resigned. The board says it acted on input from "senior levels of the United States government," and its investigation will still run to the end. (Nvidia)

📈 Anthropic raised $81B in the biggest IPO ever and opened 34% above its $83 price. ANT first traded at $111.50 and closed the day at $97.17. (Al Jazeera)

🔓 OpenRouter dropped Together AI, Phala and Novita after prompts leaked through them. Dragos sent two million test prompts carrying decoy credentials, and some of those credentials got used in the wild. (BleepingComputer)

📏 OpenAI's chief scientist wants every lab to agree on a capability line none of them cross. Jakub Pachocki would measure it by perplexity and hold it until AI behavior can be properly monitored, though he hasn't put a number on it. (AI Weekly)

⚡ Google's Gemini 4 Neon sits between Carbon and Hydrogen at $3 in and $15 out per million tokens. Google says it costs 32% less per task than Carbon. (Google)

🎟️ The bot offered a discount Fabrice Dal Maso has AI drafting replies to real customers, and one made-up discount is enough to cause a headache, so he keeps strict rules in the prompt and a person approving everything before it goes out.

👷 Eight engineers who don't exist Bhavishya Singla's LLM rewrites LinkedIn profiles and never once crashed. It just told a marketing manager to claim a team of 8 engineers and called a nurse with 7 years of experience "Aspiring."

📖 The 400-line wake-up call Abdurrahman Hakan Acar barely read his agent's code until one file passed 400 lines and he was debugging a state bug in code he'd never seen, so now he reads every diff that touches state, network calls or file storage.
