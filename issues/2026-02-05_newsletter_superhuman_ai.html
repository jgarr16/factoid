FROM THE FRONTIER

Beyond the boardroom: Some of AI’s most impressive work is happening outside the office

Made with Midjourney

Driving on Mars. Last December, NASA's Perseverance rover** followed** a 400-meter route through Martian terrain — planned entirely by Anthropic's Claude. The AI-planned path was verified through a simulation that modeled over 500,000 variables before being transmitted to Mars. Engineers estimate that using Claude can help cut route-planning time in half.

Predicting Earth’s atmosphere. Nvidia recently** unveiled** its Earth-2 family of open-source AI weather models — three new tools that forecast everything from 15-day global outlooks to zero-to-six-hour predictions of local storms in just minutes. Mike Pritchard, Nvidia's director of climate simulation, says the models are "1,000 times faster" than conventional methods. Watch Earth-2 in action here.

Monitoring the cosmos. European Space Agency astronomers** built** a neural network called AnomalyMatch and pointed it at 35 years of Hubble telescope data. In 2.5 days, it scanned nearly 100 million image cutouts and flagged roughly 1,400 anomalous objects — more than 800 of which had never been documented.

Each use case shares a common trait. AI is solving a problem that was previously limited by human time, not intelligence. Scientists already knew how to plan rover routes, forecast storms, and spot cosmic anomalies — they just couldn't do it fast enough to keep up with the data. AI isn't replacing expertise here. It's removing the bottleneck.

PROMPT STATION

The Problem Solver

Prompt: I’m stuck with [clearly describe the business problem: no customers, stalled growth, pricing pushback, delivery issues, time constraints, etc.].
Act as an experienced business consultant and operator.

First, ask me 10 sharp diagnostic questions to identify the true root cause (not symptoms). Prioritize questions around positioning, demand, distribution, pricing, operations, and constraints.

After I answer, deliver a structured analysis covering:
The core reason this problem exists (with evidence from my answers)
Three actionable solutions, ranked by expected impact vs. effort
A step-by-step execution plan for the highest-leverage solution (what to do, in what order, and why)
Systems, habits, or guardrails to prevent this issue from recurring
A clear 30-day success definition with measurable outcomes
Keep the advice practical, decisive, and operator-level (no fluff, no generic business theory). Focus on leverage and speed.
