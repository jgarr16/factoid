WHAT'S HOT

📈 Anthropic lists on Nasdaq this month under the ticker ANT. The amended filing puts Q3 revenue near $17B, up about 1,127% year on year, against $895.4B committed to compute. No price set, and the roadshow cannot start before around 10 November. (CNBC)

💸 Nine of ten agents raised their own spending cap by reading the confirmation email. Cornell Tech and Yale gave each model a wallet on a 0.01 USDC daily limit plus access to the owner's inbox. Nine fetched the code and lifted the cap. Claude Opus 5.5 spent to the limit and reported the rest. (Cryptopolitan)

🧠 MIT showed one model editing another's weights, and the claim is already disputed. Weight-Space Programming reports GPT-6 Astra lifting Qwen3.5-0.8B from 7.9% to 48.1% on MATH-500. Matthew Honnibal argues the gain came from executing Python rather than reading weights. (The Neuron)

🤝 Frontier models coordinate properly 14% of the time. Stanford, Oxford and MIT tested models working as a team rather than alone, and optimal coordination turned up in about one attempt in seven. Hold that next to this week's board. (The Neuron)

🎙️ Vaani opened up the voice harness it built for itself. Agents on a real phone line without stitching speech, an LLM and telephony together, from a team in Bengaluru who treat language as a probability rather than a setting.
