FROM THE FRONTIER

The AI boom is built on Nvidia — but the chip giant is facing threats from every direction

Made with Midjourney

Just three years ago, Nvidia was doing roughly $7B in quarterly revenue. Now it’s up to $96B and is on track to command 75% of the AI accelerator market in 2026. Nvidia’s GPUs still train and run almost every large model, but competitors are eager to disrupt its dominance:

  1. Startups are pitching updated chip designs: Chip startups raised $8.3B as of April, with many arguing GPUs were never purpose-built for AI, so leaner designs can cut costs and energy requirements.

  2. AI leaders are expanding quickly: OpenAI claims its first chip Jalapeño offers industry-leading performance, Anthropic is hiring hardware execs, and rival chipmaker Cerebras just went public in May to raise fresh funding.

  3. Big Tech is catching up: Google launched two new chips in April, Amazon is constantly improving its Trainium line, and Microsoft is expected to unveil the new Maia 300 at some point this month. That’s on top of existing competition from AMD and Broadcom.

  4. China is becoming increasingly self-reliant: Nvidia controlled 66% of China’s accelerator market in 2024, but is on track to own just 8% in 2026 as China approaches self-sufficiency.

Nvidia’s still far out in front. The chipmaker’s real moat is CUDA, the software system that millions of developers are building on. And, even as competitors ship viable alternatives, the industry is expanding faster than any single challenger can absorb.

How to turn messy files into polished Word docs with Claude

  • Go to Claude for Microsoft and install the add-in

  • Open Microsoft Word, access Claude from the sidebar and select Opus 4.6

  • Upload your messy Word file, PDF, CSV, image, or other source material and enter the prompt below:

“Prompt: Clean up this document and organize it into clear sections with headings. Fix the grammar and improve clarity. Add a summary of the key points at the top, highlight important insights or repeated ideas, and structure any data into clean tables. Then rewrite everything into a polished, professional document that’s ready to share._“ —

  • Let Claude process the content and create the polished version

  • Continue prompting Claude directly from the sidebar until it looks right

💡 Tip: Ask Claude to match a specific tone, brand style, or document format for a more tailored result.
