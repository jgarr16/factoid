FROM THE FRONTIER

Agentic adoption is slower than expected — but companies successfully scaling agents share three similar traits

Made with Midjourney

Agents are taking longer than expected to get hired. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman blamed “economic inertia” for the holdup, saying people are generally just resistant to change. While that’s likely true, there’s at least one other factor at play.

Agents are still a security risk. Today’s top models can handle more autonomy than ever, analyzing scenarios, making strategic decisions, and interacting with other systems. But as their capabilities grow, so do the consequences of a potential failure. And with 66% of companies citing security risk as their top concern, AI still seems to be waiting on the green light from corporate.

The answer may be in going niche, not broad

The growing issue is designing agent workflows that can withstand scrutiny from risk, legal, and compliance teams. Companies doing this successfully share three similarities:

  • Swapping end-to-end workflows for single-responsibility agents with a smaller scope of work.

  • Requiring agents to leave a written log of all decisions and actions.

  • Adding human review checkpoints before the agent executes any high-stakes actions.

If the agent rollout is truly slower than expected, that just means opportunity still abounds for companies able to move quickly.

TUTORIAL

How to Build and Launch a Website with ChatGPT Sites

  • Open ChatGPT desktop app and switch to the ‘Codex’ tab

  • Click on ‘Sites’ in the left panel and select ‘Create new site‘

  • Describe what you want to build, or upload a file ChatGPT can use as context

“Sample Prompt: @Sites Create a website to build an interactive project tracker for my marketing team. Include task owners, deadlines, priorities, progress, and filters for status._“ —

  • ChatGPT will generate the first version of your Site based on your instructions

  • Continue editing the Site by telling ChatGPT what to change

“Sample follow-up prompt: Add a dashboard showing completed, overdue, and upcoming tasks. Make the design cleaner and more professional._“ —

  • Test the Site in the private preview before sharing it and click ‘Publish’ when the Site is ready

  • ChatGPT will generate a shareable URL you can send to teammates, customers, or stakeholders

💡** Tip:** Return to the same project whenever you need to update the Site. Describe the changes you want, preview them, and publish the updated version.
