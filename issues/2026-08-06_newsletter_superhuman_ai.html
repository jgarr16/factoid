FROM THE FRONTIER

Agents from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta are turning into hackers

Made with Midjourney

New AI models are growing increasingly erratic

Just one week ago, an OpenAI model broke containment and tried to hack Hugging Face. The lab already experienced two more incidents since then. And it’s not alone. Anthropic and Meta both confirmed their models have gained unauthorized access to other systems during tests. Some skeptics (and memes) insist these are just publicity stunts — but major organizations are starting to sound the alarm.

Engaging in “potentially harmful” activity directed at real people

The most notable incident involved Anthropic’s Claude Mythos. The UK’s AI Security Institute documented an instance of Mythos trying to insert malicious code into an open-source project. Not only did it write and upload the code, but it also created fake online identities so it could pressure the project's maintainer to approve it.

No real harm was done, and it’s worth noting that the agent’s safeguards had been disabled for the test. But it’s still one of the first times that an agent has manifested deception this clearly, without specific prompting.

Organizations are rallying

JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon is urging industry leaders to address the risks posed by AI, per Reuters. The outlet says he has personally contacted leadership at 40 critical infrastructure companies. When one of the world's most powerful bankers starts making personal phone calls, the risk is likely very real.

How to turn your images into Canva designs

  • Go to Gemini and sign up

  • Enter your prompt to generate image

Sample Prompt: Create an image of a male tennis player reaching toward ultra-wide lens, racket in hand, giant tennis ball in foreground. Forced perspective, low angle, blue sky, outdoor court. Cinematic sports ad, vivid summer lighting, vertical 4:5

  • Once generated, type ‘@Canva make it editable’ and link your Canva account in Gemini

  • Gemini will send the image directly to Canva as an editable design

  • Open the design in Canva and customize text, graphics, colors, layouts, and other elements as needed
