NEW TOOLS

5 launches not to miss

Willo ↗ — A companion you talk to while you play WoW

Willo is a little wisp who rides along while you play World of Warcraft, so you hold your talk key, ask about a quest, some lore or where to go next, and she answers out loud in her own voice while you keep playing.

Skysoon ↗ — Beautiful weather timelines and countdowns to what's next.

Skysoon is an iPhone weather app that counts down to whatever the sky does next, so the screen says "12 min till it rains" or "8h 21m till the sun sets" and you don't have to read a chart to decide whether to walk home now.

Mister Blocks ↗ — Take a picture of your LEGO and AI will make a build

Mister Blocks is a website that looks at a photo of your loose LEGO and comes back a few minutes later with builds made from those exact bricks, each one stud-checked, with 3D step-by-step instructions you can turn and print.

ChromePing ↗ — Talk to anyone by phone, in a language you don't speak

ChromePing calls any real phone number from your browser and translates the conversation live in both directions, with captions running underneath, so whoever answers just picks up their normal phone and hears their own language a couple of seconds after you stop.

skuttr ↗ — Type one sentence, get a Reel from your camera roll

skuttr is an iPhone app that turns one sentence into a Reel, so you type "summer in Italy" or "January 1st over the years" and it goes through your camera roll, picks the moments and puts the strongest shot first, with a vertical video ready in under a minute.

WHY I BUILT THIS

A self-centered language app, a dead man’s switch, and an easier way to cancel subscriptions

We appreciate when a founder can explain the problem and the solution in a few sentences. Here are three new products that launched this week— as explained by the people who built them:

A language app that talks back

“If you're not the type of person who gets hooked on streaks, points and badges, most language apps get boring pretty quickly. … What if, instead of just having a friendly AI tutor, you could also talk to personalities who have their own opinions, moods and interests? Who care less about you and more about themselves. You know, like pretty much everyone else you talk to. That's how Kippy … came about.” – Steve Olesansky

A dead man’s switch for your phone

“A while back I hiked an active volcano alone and realized nobody would have known for days if something went wrong. So I built an app called If You Read This. You check in just by opening it. If you don't, it reminds you before your deadline, then texts the people you picked a message you wrote ahead of time.” – Ryan Mercado

A subscription-finder that doesn’t need your bank details

“We kept finding charges we'd forgotten about: a free trial that quietly became £9.99, an app nobody used anymore, a price that went up without anyone noticing. Every tracker we tried wanted a bank login first. We didn't want to give that to an app. So Subscriptrack works from your email.” – Talib Ismayilzada

PROMOTED

Nobody writes good repro steps

Record for Agents hands your coding agent the context you would otherwise have typed out. Narrate the problem on screen and Loom captures the clicks, the pages you visited and what you said about them, then writes the lot up as a structured prompt. You review and edit that before it goes anywhere, which is the part most bug reports skip. From there the agent can draft the ticket, write the code or carry out the task itself.
