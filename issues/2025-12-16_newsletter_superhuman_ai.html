How to use Adobe Photoshop, Express, and Acrobat in ChatGPT

Adobe just brought Photoshop, Express, and Acrobat into ChatGPT so you can edit photos, design visuals, and polish documents just by describing what you want. Here’s how:

  • Sign in to ChatGPT.

  • Go to “Settings” > Apps & Connectors > Adobe Photoshop/Acrobat/Express > Connect

  • Additional details can be found here: Edit with Photoshop in ChatGPT | Adobe Blog

  • For Adobe Photoshop: Upload an image and type prompts to remove objects, change backgrounds, or apply effects. Use sliders to adjust settings.

  • For Acrobat: Upload and edit PDFs, extract text, convert formats, merge/split files, compress documents, and add watermarks.

  • For Express: Search Adobe Express’ vast template library to find the best one for any moment, replace images and text, and animate elements to generate content on the fly.

FROM THE FRONTIER

Stanford researchers share their top predictions for AI in 2026

Made with Midjourney

2026 will be the year hype meets rigor. After a few years of lofty promises (and valuations), researchers at the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI) agree: 2026 will be the year of AI evaluation. The question is no longer “Can AI do this?” but “How well, at what cost, and for whom?”

AI productivity dashboards will emerge. Stanford's Erik Brynjolfsson predicts the emergence of "AI economic dashboards" that will track AI's impact in real-time to help identify exactly where AI is boosting productivity, displacing workers, or creating new roles. Stanford has already started measuring AI’s impact on employment.

Sovereign AI will reshape competition. 2026 will also be a pivotal year for countries aiming to announce their AI independence from the United States, says HAI Co-Director James Landay. He also predicts that there will not be an artificial generative intelligence (AGI) breakthrough next year.

Industry reality checks incoming. The general sentiment from Stanford's researchers is this: the age of speculative excitement for AI is coming to an end, and the age of proving its worth is just starting. This shift doesn’t signal that the AI industry is fading, just maturing.
