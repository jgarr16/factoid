TODAY IN AI

Listen to Dessa, a lifelike voice model from Jack Dorsey’s Block. Image source: Techcrunch.

1. Kimi releases an open-weight model that tops Fable and Astra: The Chinese lab unveiled Kimi K3.1 Pro, its most capable model yet that it claims excels in coding, 3D creation, and financial research. The new model tops Anthropic’s Fable 5.1 and OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra to land 4th on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Kimi also released a Highspeed version, which delivers results at 2-3x speed. Full weights are expected by November 4th. Try Kimi K3.1 Pro here.

2. Jack Dorsey’s Block drops a voice model that runs on a MacBook: Dessa was trained from scratch by listening to human conversation, as opposed to analyzing written text. This lets it hold human-like conversations full of nuances like pauses, breathing, inflections, sarcasm, and more. It’s a full-duplex model, meaning it listens and talks at the same time, and is small enough to run locally on a MacBook Pro (M4 Max or better). Listen to Dessa here.

3. Anthropic and OpenAI share security updates amid Black October: As AI continues to exploit bugs in widely used software, Anthropic showed that formal software verification could be used to rewrite programs so they have mathematically proven security guarantees. But top labs are split: While OpenAI shared steps it’s taking to secure internal training environments, CEO Sam Altman said he no longer believes we’ll be able to patch every bug.

How to automate restaurant reservation hunting with Grok Bot

Photo: Vellum

  • Open Grok Bot > New chat > Create new Bot, then name it something like Reservation Hunter.

  • In the Bot’s first message, set the job, search area, approval boundary, and what information it may use for booking.

Sample Prompt: “Find a table for [party size] at [restaurant type / specific restaurants] in [location] on [date] between [time range]. Call restaurants if online availability is unavailable or unclear, ask about cancellations or waitlist openings, and record the restaurant, available time, seating type, deposit, and cancellation policy. Keep checking until you find a suitable option. Do not book anything without my approval.”

  • Let Grok Bot search reservation sites and call restaurants that don’t show live availability online.

  • Review the shortlist it returns, including the best available times and any booking conditions.

  • Approve your preferred option and let Grok Bot complete the reservation using the details you provide.

  • Save the workflow as a Skill and add a recurring Routine if you want it to keep checking for earlier or better cancellation slots.

FROM THE FRONTIER

Want to sign up for an agent but don’t have time to research them? Here’s what you should know.

Made with Midjourney

Picking an agent is a much bigger deal than it seems. It’s not just another software tool, it’s a long-term teammate that’ll either take your productivity to new highs — or be a daily source of frustration. Here’s each agent’s reputation (in no particular order).

Anthropic’s Claude Duet: The new contender powered by Opus 5.5

Claude Duet is built for professional use, excelling at small tasks, large projects, and multitasking. The upside: it’s powered by the top overall model across most rankings, which can fuel better quality. The downside: you need a Pro or Max plan (starts at $20/month), and you have to join a waitlist.

Meta’s Muse: The App Store leader that scans social media

Muse is known for its customization and is mainly used for personal tasks like shopping or booking reservations. The upside: it has a free tier, a playful design, and access to Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. The downside: it may fall short on more complex use cases.

OpenAI’s dots: The good listener that’s professional but casual

Dots strikes a middle ground between professional and personal use. It leverages OpenAI’s leading voice and computer abilities to do great work, and adds a cute personality to keep things fun. The upside: it excels at receiving voice commands. The downside: only one dot is included in Pro or Business Premium plans (starts at $100/month)

xAI’s Grok Bot: The agent that works well with teammates

Grok Bot offers multi-bot coordination, making it easy to spin up entire teams. Think: one bot monitoring emails, one tackling projects, and one managing the others. The upside: Grok Bot can monitor X. The downside: it requires a premium plan (starts at $30/month).
