FROM THE FRONTIER

Hollywood is warming up to AI — as long as it’s on their terms.

Made with Midjourney

Hollywood spent 2023 fighting AI — but now some of its biggest names are joining its cast.

Disney’s game-changing deal. The entertainment giant recently announced a $1 billion equity investment in OpenAI, allowing Sora users to create videos with 200+ copyrighted characters — shocking many in the industry. CEO Bob Iger sees AI as inevitable, saying, "No human generation has ever stood in the way of technological advance, and we don't intend to try."

A-listers are monetizing their voices. Many prominent actors — including Matthew McConaughey and Michael Caine — have partnered with AI audio company ElevenLabs to license virtual replications of their voices. Michael Caine’s opinion? ElevenLabs is "using innovation not to replace humanity, but to celebrate it."

Even AI critics see opportunity. James Cameron (the man who literally warned us about killer robots) has been vocal about AI's threat to the industry. Yet he's also joined Stability AI's board with a specific mission: to build AI tools that help VFX artists work faster, not replace them. He believes there are lots of AI products for consumers — but few built for filmmakers.

Lights, camera, algorithm. Hollywood isn't just flirting with AI anymore. While the lawyers are arguing over the fine print, the bean counters have already seen the math, and it's too good to pass up. Whether this leads to a creative boom or just a lot of uncanny valley cameos, the industry is betting its future on silicon over sets.

PROMPT STATION

Focus on what matters

You are a ruthless prioritization coach with zero tolerance for fluff.
If I had to kill this idea today — [insert idea] — what is the single most valuable thing I would regret losing?
Strip everything else away. Identify the core insight, asset, or advantage that is truly irreplaceable.
Explain why this element matters, what makes it unique, and why it would be hardest (or impossible) to recreate later.
If nothing is worth regretting, say so plainly.
