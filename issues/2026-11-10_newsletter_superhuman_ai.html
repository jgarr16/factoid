FROM THE FRONTIER

AI overuse could be creating a “speed trap” at your company. Here’s how to avoid it.

Made with Midjourney

A “speed trap” is when AI churns out work faster than anyone can understand it. The productivity boost feels great at first. But some leaders say it leads to more confusion down the road.

At Every’s Thesis: 2027 conference, Notion CEO Ivan Zhao warned that a codebase can stop making sense within just one week of engineers looping in their own coding agents. A viral Reddit post claims this exact scenario played out at their company: their codebase tripled in size, but now no one understands it. It’s not just engineers, either.

Steve Huynh, a former Amazon principal engineer, argued that reliance on AI is threatening the company’s writing culture, potentially weakening decision-making.

How can you avoid speed traps?

  1. Keep learning in the loop: Anthropic recommends designing workflows so employees keep learning and retain the skills necessary to fact-check AI’s outputs.

  2. Always add your own perspective: Every CEO Dan Shipper argues anyone can get a default AI answer. The real value is the perspective you add on top of it, and that only comes from your understanding of the work itself.

  3. Make authors defend their work: Mathematician Terence Tao says if authors can’t give a clear, expert-level talk on an AI-assisted result, it shouldn’t be published. Consider holding your team’s memos, decks, and code to the same bar.
