How provider data policies work on OpenRouter

Hi John,

Every provider behind OpenRouter has its own retention and training policy. OpenRouter reflects those policies as structured data on each endpoint, publishes the full table in the docs, and gives you controls to filter routing on them.

View the provider policy table

WHAT YOU CONTROL

1. Block routing to providers that train. Your privacy settings have an account-wide toggle for whether routing may use providers that train on prompts, with separate switches for paid and free models. If you opt out, OpenRouter will not route to training providers.

2. Require a specific data policy per request. You can override your account default on any single call. See data policy filtering in the provider routing docs.

3. Pick only zero-retention providers. Many providers on OpenRouter are zero retention (Amazon Bedrock, Azure, Cerebras, DeepInfra, Google Vertex, Groq, NVIDIA, SambaNova, Together, and others in the table). Filter to them the same way as training policy.

IF YOU NEED STRICTER GUARANTEES

OpenRouter supports EU in-region routing for enterprise accounts. When enabled, prompts and completions are processed inside the European Union via the https://eu.openrouter.ai base URL. Contact the enterprise team to enable it.
