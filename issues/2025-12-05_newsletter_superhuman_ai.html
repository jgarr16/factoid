Mastering AI isn't just about prompts — you also need to understand which models to prompt

Made with Midjourney

AI models are getting increasingly specialized — and choosing the right model is becoming critical for generating the best results.

Different models have different skills. AI training has two phases: Pre-training, where models absorb foundational knowledge from massive datasets, and post-training, when they’re refined for specific tasks. Since each model receives unique training, they each end up with unique specialties.

Meet the writing and coding specialists. Experts consider Gemini Pro 3, Grok 4.1 Thinking, and Claude Sonnet 4.5 to be the best wordsmiths, depending on the type of writing you need. For coding, Claude Opus 4.5 outranks competitors — scoring the highest on SWE-bench Verified for real-world software engineering.

Meet the image and video experts. Gemini-powered Nano Banana Pro leads the way for image generation and editing. Meanwhile, Runway Gen-4.5 — released earlier this week — outranks Veo 3, Kling 2.5, and Sora 2 Pro as the best text-to-video model.

Build your AI superteam. As AI continues to advance, matching the right model to the task at hand will be crucial for generating the best results. These models listed above currently lead in their respective categories, but the ranks could change at any moment — even as early as next week.

The Format Flip

Your output should be complete, polished, and implementation-ready, containing every component typically required for this format — structure, wording, layout, and any placeholders.

Follow these rules:
No commentary — deliver only the final formatted output.
Fill in all logical details unless I specify placeholders.
Make it clean, professional, and consistent, so I can copy-paste and use it immediately with zero edits.

Inputs:
Format type: [specific format]
Intended use: [purpose]

Output:
A complete, ready-to-deploy version of the requested format.
