FROM THE FRONTIER

Choose your company’s AI stance fast — before your customers choose for you.

The anti-AI consumer. Half of US consumers would rather buy from brands that don't use generative AI in ads, messaging, or content — and companies are starting to pick sides. "Made by humans" is quickly becoming the next organic, non-GMO, or handmade.

Leading the charge. Companies like Aerie, Equinox, Almond Breeze, and Polaroid have turned “anti-AI” into a marketing strategy — from public callouts to full-blown ad campaigns. Gartner analyst Emily Weiss puts it bluntly: "marketers should treat GenAI as a trust decision as much as a technology decision."

But there’s a catch. The "No AI" badge isn’t about avoiding AI completely — it’s about being transparent with customers. Using AI to create a simple background? Only 4% think that needs a label. But using an AI voice to chat with customers? 96% say that needs a disclosure. When in doubt, just remember this rule of thumb: don't pretend a machine is a human.

Pick your lane. Brands using AI unapologetically are winning on volume, speed, and cost. Those staying deliberately human-first are winning on customer goodwill. But the ones in the middle? They’re slowly losing on both ends.

How to turn any flat image into an editable design

  • Open Canva → go to Magic Layers, click Select Media, and upload your image

  • Wait ~30– 60 seconds. Canva automatically detects layout, text, objects, and background, and separates everything into editable layers

  • Click any layer to edit instantly. Update text, fix typos, change dates, or tweak taglines without rebuilding the design

  • Select object layers to move, resize, duplicate, or remove elements with full control

  • Drag in a new image (product shot, logo, etc.). It snaps into the existing layout automatically

  • Fine-tune the design by adjusting colors using the color picker to match your brand or style
