FROM THE FRONTIER

Nvidia’s CEO eyes $1T in chip sales and agents-as-a-service

Made with Midjourney

Nvidia GTC has become one of the AI industry’s most exciting events — and this year’s conference is no exception.

The headline number. $1 trillion. That’s how much Nvidia plans to generate in Blackwell and Vera Rubin chips sales through 2027 — up $500B from its previous forecast. It's a major signal that enterprise AI investment isn't slowing down, and the chip maker plans to take full advantage.

Introducing NemoClaw. The viral tool OpenClaw played a major role in this year’s keynote, with Huang calling it “the most popular open source project in the history of humanity." Nvidia plans to make the tool more enterprise-friendly with NemoClaw: a software stack that helps companies safely build and deploy OpenClaw agents.

Key hardware announcements. Nvidia also revealed Vera Rubin, a seven-chip supercomputer that Huang said will fuel "the greatest infrastructure buildout in history” along with the Space-1 — Nvidia’s platform to put data centers into orbit. Its engineers are already working on one core challenge: cooling a supercomputer when faced with radiation.

The agent economy. Huang ended his keynote with a bold prediction: Software-as-a-service (SaaS) will slowly become agents-as-a-service (AGaas) — with OpenClaw and NemoClaw serving as the foundation. Only time will tell if this vision is foresight…or false hype. Watch the full keynote in 12 minutes.

THE AI ACADEMY

How to perfectly lip sync your videos with AI

  • Go to Freepik and sign up

  • Go to Video > Tools > Speak

  • Start with an image or video as the base for your speaking clip

  • Describe what the character says and how they say it

  • Pick a suitable voice for your character

  • Add your own audio to give your video an authentic voice and click ‘Generate’

  • Wait for a few seconds and you’ll get a lip-synced talking video
