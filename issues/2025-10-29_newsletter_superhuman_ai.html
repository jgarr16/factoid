FROM THE FRONTIER

Adobe launches AI image model and other upgrades to power content creation

Source: Adobe

Adobe’s annual design conference kicked off in Los Angeles last night, where the company announced an array of current and future updates to its suite of creative tools.

Some of the biggest product updates from the night:

  • Firefly Image 5: A new model that creates native 4MP images with photorealistic lighting and texture. It features layered editing, a tool that treats objects as separate layers, allowing you to edit them individually without compromising the image.

  • Firefly Audio Tools: The update lets you generate commercially safe background music directly in Firefly. You can also create voiceovers in over 15 languages using Adobe and ElevenLabs models.

  • 'Generative Fill' with Partner Models: Photoshop now lets you choose from third-party AI models — including Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, Black Forest Labs Kontext, and Firefly — to edit or modify content with simple text prompts.

  • Firefly Custom Models: You can create your own personalized AI model by simply dragging and dropping your images into Firefly. It'll learn your unique style and generate assets with visual consistency across all your work.

  • Integration with ChatGPT (coming soon): The move will integrate Adobe's creative features directly into ChatGPT, allowing users to toggle between both apps.

THE AI ACADEMY

How to turn AI research into slides in minutes

  • Run a deep research query in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini.

  • Copy the full output and paste it into ChatGPT with this prompt:

Sample Prompt: “Rewrite as a JSON file, preserving all style and structure elements.”

  • Paste the JSON back into ChatGPT and prompt: “Restructure this JSON for presentation slides.”

  • The AI will condense text into concise, slide-ready sections. Review for balance.

  • Copy the final JSON and open Gamma, Canva, or PowerPoint AI.

  • Create a new presentation, paste the JSON, and prompt: “Use this JSON to generate professional slides.”

  • Choose a theme that fits your brand.

  • Review slides for clarity and flow. Replace stock visuals with AI or branded images.

  • Adjust fonts, spacing, and colors as needed — then export and share.

PROMPT STATION

Academic Research Deep Dive

Prompt: I’m researching [specific topic or concept]. Please help me map the academic landscape around it by identifying and analyzing key works.

Specifically:
Identify foundational and influential academic or philosophical studies that:
Explore [topic] directly
Connect through related or underlying concepts
Offer fresh, interdisciplinary, or unexpected perspectives
For each major work, include:
Its central argument or finding
Why it matters to understand my topic
Any distinctive methodology or theoretical lens used
Its influence on later research or public thought

Highlight:
Surprising or novel links between disciplines
New frameworks that shift how we think about the topic
Contrarian or underexplored perspectives that challenge the mainstream view
Lesser-known works that offer deep or original insights
Summarize any major debates or contrasting schools of thought within the literature.

Finally, recommend the best entry points for deeper exploration based on my particular interest in [specific aspect of the topic].
