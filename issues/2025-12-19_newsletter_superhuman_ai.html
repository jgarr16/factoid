FROM THE FRONTIER

Claude has been running a small business for 7 months. Here’s how it’s doing.

Made with Midjourney

What’s Project Vend? Since June, Anthropic has been running an experiment: letting Claude manage real vending machine stores in their offices. The AI — nicknamed "Claudius" — was given a simple prompt to handle the entire business. The goal? To test AI’s ability to successfully manage economic resources.

The disaster phase. Early results weren’t very good. Claudius bled cash. Employees pranked it easily, bad supplier deals tanked profits, and it kept selling inventory at a loss. While the model was intelligent, it struggled to handle every task on its own. The model also went out of its way to help people, which isn’t a recipe for business success.

The turnaround. A major turning point occurred when Claude added some extra support: a CRM system, better inventory tracking, web browsing to compare suppliers, and even other AI employees to help run the store. The results were dramatic. Efficiency soared, and Claudius started hitting consistently profitable months in late 2025.

The insight. As companies plug AI into increasingly critical functions, we need real-world data to design guardrails that protect against disasters without killing the models' usefulness. A major challenge ahead isn’t about making AI smarter — it's about learning exactly where humans still need to step in. Watch a summary of Project Vend here.

PROMPT STATION

10-Sec Resume Test

Prompt: Act as a senior recruiter and hiring manager reviewing the first page of my resume for the role of {role} (Job Description attached).

You have 10 seconds to scan the resume, as you would in a high-volume hiring process with strong competition.

Please evaluate:

Immediate attention drivers: What sections, bullets, and keywords stand out first— and why?

Role alignment: How clearly does my experience map to the job requirements and success criteria in the JD?

Keyword & signal strength: Which required or high-value keywords are present, missing, weak, or misaligned (including ATS-relevant terms)?

Differentiation: What makes me competitive— or forgettable— compared to other strong candidates?

Red flags or friction points: Anything that causes hesitation, confusion, or skepticism.

Interview likelihood: Estimate the probability (0– 100%) that you would advance me to an interview, and explain the rationale.

Be ruthlessly honest and assume the bar is high.
Conclude with specific, prioritized changes that would most improve my interview callback rate.
