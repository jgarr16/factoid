TUTORIAL

How to do a digital declutter with Grok bot

Source: petergyang

  • Go to Grok Bot, download the desktop app, and sign in

  • Go to Plugin > connect your Gmail and Google Drive

  • Click the “+” icon in the top-left corner > Create new Bot

  • Describe what you want the Bot to do in plain language. Grok will use your instructions to set up the Bot for your workflow

  • Once it’s ready, name your Bot so it’s easy to identify from the sidebar

“Sample Prompt: "You are Marie Kondo for cleaning up digital clutter. Audit my email, Google Drive, and paid subscriptions, then create a cleanup plan with no more than 10 items per category. Find emails to unsubscribe from, redundant files to remove or rename, and paid subscriptions I may want to cancel. Do not move, delete, unsubscribe, or cancel anything without my approval."_“ —
