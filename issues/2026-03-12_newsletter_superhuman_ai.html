FROM THE FRONTIER

There’s a sweet spot for AI usage — and it’s probably a lot less than you think

Made with Midjourney

AI productivity study. ActivTrak just published a massive workplace productivity study, spanning 1,111 companies and 160,000+ employees. The data revealed something counterintuitive: there’s an optimal usage for AI — and it’s not looping Claude into every conversation.

Intensifying workloads. The study found that AI is intensifying work, not lightening it. Employees who actively use AI tools saw spikes in email time, business management tool usage, and even weekend productivity. It’s clear that high-performing employees are using AI to do more work, not the same amount more efficiently. But AI-fueled productivity also hits a limit.

The sweet spot. Employees who spent 7– 10% of their work hours using AI tools had a productivity rate of 95% — higher than both non-adopters and super-users. That’s roughly 50 minutes per day, assuming an 8-hour workday. The problem: Only 3% of users landed in that sweet spot.

The secret formula? ActivTrak's data suggests that employees who refuse to try AI are undoubtedly falling behind. But if you find yourself tabbing to ChatGPT every 10 minutes, you might be better off saving it for high-value projects — and handling the small things yourself.
