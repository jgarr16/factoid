TUTORIAL

How to automate new-hire onboarding with Grok Bot

Photo: SpaceXAI

  • Open the Grok Bot app and add the Onboarding Manager from SpaceXAI’s Use Cases page. You can also create one manually via New > Create new Bot.

  • Connect the systems containing your onboarding material at Connectors, such as Google Drive, Notion, SharePoint, Outlook, or Microsoft Teams.

  • Give the Bot access to your onboarding checklist, employee handbook, IT setup guides, policies, team docs, and other approved resources.

  • Give it a clear onboarding brief for each new hire.

Sample prompt: “Onboard [Name], our new [Role], starting [Date]. Build a checklist for their first week using our approved onboarding documents. Pull the relevant policies, tools, training, and team resources into one place. Answer questions only from those sources, flag anything unclear, and identify blockers that need HR, IT, or their manager. Do not send messages, create accounts, or make changes without approval.”

  • Test the Bot with real questions. Correct any mistakes. Then ask Grok Bot to save it as a Skill so the same onboarding process can be reused for future hires.
