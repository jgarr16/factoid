🪐 OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra

Welcome back, Superhuman. We may have just entered the age of AGI (the period where AI finally exceeds human intelligence). That’s not our prediction. It’s a quote from OpenAI’s president, based on how powerful the lab’s latest GPT-6 Astra model is. We’ve shared some of the best use cases below.

Today: Google Workspace gets voice control, how to let Claude complete tasks on your computer, and get the latest prompts and trending social posts.

TODAY IN AI

Click to watch OpenAI’s new GPT-6 Astra model in action. Source: OpenAI

1. OpenAI says we’ve entered the AGI era with its latest model: OpenAI’s president, Greg Brockman, says the lab’s GPT-6 Astra model represents a “generational leap” in capabilities, and its release could mark the moment AI starts to match and exceed human reasoning. The model is being rolled out slowly to address any cyber security concerns, but should be generally available in the coming days. Learn the best practices for prompting Astra.

2. Early testers share Astra use cases, but many still have no access:

  • Matt Shumer built a virtual world Manhattan in Unreal Engine

  • Pietro Schirano built animation in code from an image

  • Riley Brown shared his favorite productivity prompt

  • Claire Vo shared an in-depth 30 minute video with several use cases

However, many users are claiming they have no access to the model yet. OpenAI confirmed the issue and said paid users will get limit resets for each day they don’t have access to Astra.

3. Google rolls out voice control for Workspace, plus a new weather model: Google Workspace now offers voice-activated features across Gmail, Docs, and Keep, letting you search for emails, draft documents, or take notes by talking. The tech leaders also released WeatherNext 3, its flagship weather model that pulls real-time satellite data to offer hyper-local forecasts.

TUTORIAL

How to let Claude complete tasks on your computer

Source: Claude

  • Download the latest version of the Claude desktop app.

  • Open the app and go to Settings > General.

  • Find the ‘Enable computer’ use toggle and turn it on.

  • Open Cowork or Claude Code in the desktop app and start a session.

  • Ask Claude to do something that involves an app on your computer. Claude will ask for permission to access the app before proceeding.
