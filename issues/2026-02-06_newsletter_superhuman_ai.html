TODAY IN AI

Click to watch Claude Opus 4.6’s release video.

1. Anthropic launches Claude Opus 4.6 as a premier model for enterprise knowledge work: Anthropic released Opus 4.6, its most advanced AI model yet, achieving top scores for coding, financial analysis, and legal reasoning — all skills that are available within Claude Cowork. The model plans its responses better, needs less guidance for complicated workflows, and has a 1M context window for longer sessions. Within Claude Code, a key new feature is “agent teams”: a research preview that lets multiple AI agents work on the same task. Instead of prompting a single assistant step by step, you hand it a goal and let a team of agents plan, execute, and deliver. Available now in Claude. Watch the release video here.

2. OpenAI launches GPT-5.3 Codex and OpenAI Frontier: OpenAI dropped GPT-5.3 Codex, its most capable coding model yet, setting new highs on SWE-Bench Pro (57%) and Terminal-Bench 2.0 (77%) while running 25% faster and using fewer tokens than any prior model. The AI leader also announced OpenAI Frontier: a new platform that helps enterprises build, deploy, and manage AI agents that can do real work. The platform gives agents shared context, onboarding, and clear permissions to help them work more efficiently. Learn more about Frontier here.

3. Perplexity launches Model Council to help compare answers across AI models: Perplexity launched Model Council, a new feature that runs your query across three frontier AI models simultaneously (such as Claude Opus 4.5, GPT-5.2, and Gemini 3 Pro) then uses a synthesizer model to review all three outputs, resolve conflicts, and deliver one comprehensive answer. This feature can help users confirm and compare the validity of answers generated across frontier models. Watch Model Council in action here.

Alphabet crushes earnings as AI fuels almost every business line

Made with Midjourney

Alphabet crushes earnings. Alphabet just became a $400-billion-a-year company. Its services division drove roughly $350B, Cloud contributed $70B in annual run rate, and YouTube generated $60B on its own (larger than Netflix's entire business). Alphabet’s business is on fire — and AI is playing the role of gasoline.

Gemini: the growth engine. CEO Sundar Pichai called Gemini 3 a “major milestone” — and the numbers back it up. The model now processes over 10 billion tokens per minute, the Gemini App has surpassed 750 million monthly users, and AI-powered Search is seeing record usage. But there was one AI initiative that Alphabet was hesitant to speak about.

The Apple partnership. In January, Apple chose Google as its preferred cloud provider and announced a multi-year deal to build the next generation of Apple Foundation Models on Gemini, including a major Siri overhaul coming this year. Leadership didn’t share a single detail, which suggests that the terms are still being finalized.

Google backs Big Tech’s vision. Almost every major tech company is doubling down on AI spending this year — Alphabet is no exception. The company plans to spend up to $185 billion on capital expenditures in 2026 (largely for AI infrastructure), roughly double the $91 billion it spent in 2025.

PROMPT STATION

Cover Letter Generator

Prompt: Write a 4– 5 sentence cover letter tailored specifically to the following job description: [paste job description].

Requirements:
Personalize it to the role and company (no generic language).
Reference one concrete, measurable achievement that aligns directly with the job’s core requirement (use numbers, outcomes, or impact).
Clearly connect my skills or experience to one key problem this role is meant to solve
Use confident, concise language— professional but human.
End with a strong, proactive call to action (e.g., interest in discussing how I can contribute, not “looking forward to hearing from you”).
Avoid buzzwords, clichés, and long introductions. Every sentence should add signal and credibility.
