Workshop video: Finding hidden connections with digital ad data

During our September members-only workshop, I showed how investigators can use digital ad data to reveal hidden connections between websites and apps. I was joined by Zach Edwards, co-founder of DecryptAds and a top-notch ad investigator, who demonstrated how he uses the platform to uncover networks and dig into shady ad supply chains.

I think ads are one of the least understood and utilized areas of OSINT. This workshop showcased some of the things you can discover from examining a website’s advertising relationships. The workshop was partly based on my recent piece, “The Indicator Guide to investigating digital ad supply chains.”
