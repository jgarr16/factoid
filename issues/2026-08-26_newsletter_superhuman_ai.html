The OpenClaw effect: One of Apple’s least-marketed products just got a major revamp

Made with Midjourney

February’s OpenClaw frenzy triggered a run on Mac minis, with AI hobbyists scooping up the relatively cheap computer to run agents. Fast forward six months, and Apple has positioned the entire product line around that use case. Meet the lineup of “beefier” Mac minis targeting a new type of customer. Here are the tech specs to know:

  1. The Mac mini, powered by an M6 chip for $899 or the M5 Pro at $1,699.

  2. The Mac Studio, powered by an M5 Max chip for $2,499 or the M5 Ultra at $5,499.

The beefed-up Mac mini targets a new type of customer

AI power users face a dilemma. Running frontier models has grown expensive, but the solution — running an open weight model locally — often requires heavy-duty tech. Apple is positioning its new Mac mini to fill that gap, blurring the line between consumer and commercial compute. And it’s not the only one.

Perplexity and Nvidia just teamed up to launch Portable Computer, a local agent system that runs on Nvidia’s DGX Spark, while Darkbloom lets you rent out idle Mac compute for AI usage on its marketplace.

How to optimize your LinkedIn profile with Gemini

  • Go to your LinkedIn profile → Click More → Select ‘Save to PDF’.

  • Open Gemini, select the Pro model, and enable ‘Canvas ‘ and ‘Thinking’.

  • Upload your profile PDF and enter the prompt

“Sample Prompt: [upload profile] Before rebuilding and optimizing my LinkedIn profile, ask me a few clarifying questions. Request my primary goal (sales calls, leads, job offers, newsletter growth, authority positioning, etc.). Ask for my target audience (be specific). Ask for my main offer (what I want people to buy, book, or join). Ask for up to two Featured links (URL + description). Ask for key social proof to highlight (audience size, revenue, brands, media mentions, etc.). Once you have this, rewrite my full LinkedIn profile including headline, About section, and experience._“ —

  • Answer the questions provided and Gemini rewrites your entire profile including new headline, about section, and experience

  • Select sections and click Extend to make edits

  • Copy the optimized sections back into LinkedIn
