How to automate your Monday morning work brief with ChatGPT Work

Photo: OpenAI

  • Open ChatGPT and toggle on Work.

  • Go to Plugins and connect the work sources you want to use, such as Gmail or Slack.

  • Start a new Work chat and tell ChatGPT exactly what information should appear in your Monday brief.

Sample prompt: “Every Monday at [add time], create my weekly work brief. Review relevant activity from the previous week in [Gmail/Slack/Teams]. Summarize important decisions, unfinished tasks, requests that need a response, blockers, and anything I should follow up on. Organize the brief into: Top Priorities, Follow-Ups, Decisions, Blockers, and Recommended Next Actions. Schedule the task for every Monday at [your preferred time].”

  • Review the proposed instructions and schedule, then confirm the task.

  • Turn on email or push notifications under Settings > Notifications so the finished brief reaches you automatically each Monday.
