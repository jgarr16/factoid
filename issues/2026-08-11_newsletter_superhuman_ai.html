FROM THE FRONTIER

Last week may have been detrimental for Google’s frontier ambitions. Where does the tech giant go from here?

Made with Midjourney

Heading for the door

Google’s talent exodus last week was bad enough for SemiAnalysis to declare that Gemini is officially “cooked.” The departure list now includes Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, Oriol Vinyals, Noam Shazeer, and John Jumper. These aren’t employees who can just be replaced during the next recruiting cycle.

Jeff Dean was the company’s Chief Scientist, Sanjay and Quoc were in the company’s top dozen technical contributors, and Noam Shazeer is the self-proclaimed “inventor of the LLM revolution.” Many of them worked at Google for over a decade, which raises a question.

Why is top talent deciding to leave?

According to some reports: bureaucracy. As a major internet infrastructure provider, Google requires multiple stakeholders to sign off on new models, and getting leadership aligned can reportedly be challenging. That may be why Gemini 3.5 Pro is months behind schedule. But the tech giant hasn’t been standing still.

Alphabet may just be running a different race

Google has been spending aggressively on AI infrastructure and, so far, that strategy has paid off. Google Cloud revenue hit $24B last quarter, growing 82% year over year — roughly double the rate of AWS and Azure — while its cloud backlog ballooned to $514B. While it may be facing retention challenges, Google has still positioned itself as a core provider for the AI economy.

How to delegate work in Slack with Claude Tag

  • Open Claude Tag, click Add to Slack, and complete the setup

  • Grant Claude access to selected channels. Open the Slack channel where you want Claude to help

  • Type @Claude followed by the task and give enough context

  • Keep working while Claude researches, drafts, summarizes, or completes the task

  • Review Claude's response, make any edits, and use the output however you need

“Example prompt: @Claude Review the last week's product discussion in this channel. Summarize the key decisions, list any open action items, identify blockers, and draft a Slack update I can share with leadership._“ —

Pro tip: Treat Claude like a coworker, not a chatbot. Assign complete tasks with clear outcomes, and include any context or tool access it needs to finish the job.
