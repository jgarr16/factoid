FROM THE FRONTIER

The infrastructure reality behind OpenAI and Anthropic nobody talks about

Click here to watch the entire conversation

As CEO of ClickHouse, Aaron Katz has turned a Yandex spinout into a $6B company powering the infrastructure behind OpenAI, Anthropic, and the entire AI stack. We sat down with Katz to talk about what's really happening behind the scenes of the AI boom.

Here are the key takeaways from our conversation.

AI infrastructure is operating at an unreal scale. Tesla, for example, ingests about 1B events per second with unlimited retention. Most databases force companies to set strict retention limits, but modern AI workloads demand everything be retained. This isn't just a Tesla problem, it's the new baseline for any company building serious AI products.

A single ChatGPT prompt triggers up to 50 database queries. When you ask an AI agent a natural language question, the system writes a query, interprets results, writes another query, and repeats. "That's where low latency characteristics really put it apart from others to be the backend to a lot of these LLM providers," Katz explained.

The entire business intelligence sector is ripe for disruption. Katz claims to have built an internal system where anyone can ask, "What are the top ten customers that joined AWS this month in financial services?" and get answers in seconds. Static dashboards can't keep up with AI agents that can generate insights on demand.
