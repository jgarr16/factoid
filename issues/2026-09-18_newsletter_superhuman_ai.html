How to turn one product photo into a campaign asset kit with ChatGPT

  • Open ChatGPT > Images.

  • Upload a clean product photo with the logo and packaging clearly visible.

  • Ask ChatGPT to build a master campaign visual around the product. Give it a clear creative direction, including the mood, setting, lighting, and the amount of empty space you want for copy.

Sample Prompt: “Use this product image as the hero of a premium summer campaign. Preserve the exact packaging, logo, colors, and proportions. Place it in warm natural light with subtle organic textures and minimal props. Keep the composition clean, high-end, and realistic, with negative space on the left for ad copy. Create a polished commercial photography look suitable for a major brand campaign.”

  • Generate 2– 3 visual directions and choose one as the master creative. Refine it with small targeted edits.

  • Turn the approved visual into multiple formats using the aspect-ratio controls: 1:1 for feeds, 4:5 for Instagram, 9:16 for Stories/Reels, and 16:9 for banners.

  • Create campaign variants from the same visual system: launch ad, testimonial graphic, sale creative, product-only image, and CTA version.

  • Ask for a transparent-background product cutout so the same product can be reused in decks, landing pages, emails, and future campaigns.
