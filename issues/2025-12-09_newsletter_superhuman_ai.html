FROM THE FRONTIER

Researchers analyzed 306 deployed AI agents — and identified a roadmap for success.

Made by Midjourney

Successfully deploying AI agents. Researchers recently released the largest study of AI agents in action — surveying 306 practitioners across 26 domains. The bad news? AI isn’t ready to eliminate the world’s busy work just yet. The good news? A clear strategy for success is emerging.

Keep it simple and controlled. Nearly half of deployed agents execute fewer than five steps before requiring human intervention. 68% max out at ten steps. Why the constraints? Keeping workflows short and well-scoped enables teams to verify their outputs and ensure reliability.

Off-the-shelf models dominate. Deploying agents also doesn’t require sophisticated proprietary technology. Non-weighted versions of Claude Sonnet 4, Opus 4.1, and GPT o3 powered 17 of 20 case studies — not because they're the most advanced models, but because they're predictable.

The roadmap to success. Start with one high-value workflow, build tight constraints, and keep humans in the loop. At this phase, most companies aren’t aiming for full autonomy — they’re starting small, making steady progress, and measuring results.
