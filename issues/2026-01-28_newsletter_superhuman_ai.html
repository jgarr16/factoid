FROM THE FRONTIER

Anthropic’s CEO thinks AI is about to “test who we are as a species” — and issues several dire warnings

Made with Midjourney

Dario Amodei's 19,000-word warning. Anthropic’s CEO published "The Adolescence of Technology" this week, a follow-up to his 2024 essay Machines of Loving Grace. He argues that AI going rogue is certainly a major risk. But there’s a threat that deserves even more attention — humans using AI against other humans.

AI’s many risks. Amodei warns that AI could amplify destruction if it falls into the wrong hands — like enabling global totalitarian dictatorships or teaching ordinary people how to build bioweapons. He even thinks AI companies pose a risk, pointing out that they could wield their chatbots to "brainwash" users.

His proposed solutions. Amodei offers several solutions: transparency laws, export controls on advanced chips, mandatory disclosures about model behavior, and regulation that buys time without freezing progress. He also pledges that all Anthropic co-founders will donate 80% of their wealth to address AI-driven economic disruption.

But the messenger complicates the message. Critics have pointed out the irony: Amodei warns about both the potential power and danger of AI while also…leading an AI company. Whether the essay reflects genuine alarm, strategic positioning, or both is a question readers will have to answer for themselves. Read it here.
