Google It, or ChatGPT It?

Via CNBC

OpenAI revealed that ChatGPT now handles roughly one-fifth as many search-like queries as Google, a sign of just how quickly chatbots are changing the way people find information online.

By the numbers: ChatGPT consumer product lead Adam Fry shared the figure at an OpenAI event this week. The company now has 1.2 billion weekly active users, up 200 million in just two months. Google still processes vastly more searches, but ChatGPT’s reported volume is impressive for a product that launched only four years ago.

The fine print: OpenAI defines a search as a question that triggers a web search inside ChatGPT, but it hasn’t disclosed its exact query totals or the Google numbers used for comparison. The 20% figure is OpenAI’s estimate, not an independently verified market share statistic. Still, the trend is difficult to ignore, especially as Google increasingly incorporates AI Overviews and AI Mode into its own search experience.

The content problem: The shift is happening as websites— the ones from which chatbots pull answers— become increasingly protective of their content. Reddit is moving toward requiring users to log in, partly to limit unauthorized scraping, while The New York Times and other publishers recently sued Brave over allegations that it collected and distributed copyrighted material to AI companies. As AI-generated answers become more popular, publishers are questioning how much value they’re getting in return.

“The bigger picture: We’re moving from searching for information to asking AI to find and synthesize it for us. Even Google is pushing users toward AI-generated answers instead of traditional links. But that creates a complicated question: what happens to the websites, publishers, and communities producing all that information if people stop visiting them? AI search needs a healthy internet to draw from, and the economics of that relationship are far from settled.“ —

Apple Opens Siri AI to Third-Party Apps

Via Fortune

Apple announced that developers can now integrate Siri AI’s advanced capabilities into their own apps, allowing the assistant to perform more complex tasks without users constantly switching between applications.

What’s changing: The new developer preview, available through the iOS 27.3 beta and corresponding Apple software updates, expands Siri’s ability to understand personal context, recognize what’s happening on screen, and take actions inside third-party apps.

What Siri can do: Developers can use Apple’s App Intents framework to connect their apps to Siri and create more complex workflows. For example:

  • Ride-sharing: Ask Siri to change your pickup location while tracking an active ride, without opening the app.

  • Shopping and orders: Check deliveries, place orders, or book reservations through supported apps.

  • Multi-step tasks: Use new app skills to combine several actions into a single request.

A more personal assistant: Apple is also expanding Siri’s capabilities across its own apps, including Mail, Messages, Calendar, Photos, and Reminders. Users can ask Siri to find a coffee shop they visited last week or archive files they haven’t opened in over a year. The third-party integrations are currently in developer preview, with broader availability expected in the coming months.

“Why it matters: AI assistants are moving beyond answering questions and toward actually doing things across the apps people already use. Apple has a major advantage here because Siri is built directly into its devices and operating systems. The more context assistants can understand and the more apps they can control, the less users need to think about which app to open.“ —

Turn Your Product Into an Interactive Demo

Via AtriumKit

AtriumKit is a Chrome-based tool that records your interactions with a web app and turns them into interactive product demonstrations. It creates guided walkthroughs with tooltips and branching paths, as well as clickable sandboxes that let viewers explore products independently.

How you can use it

  • Record product demos by clicking through your web app

  • Create guided walkthroughs and interactive sandboxes

  • Generate AI voiceovers and multilingual captions

  • Embed demos in onboarding pages, help centers, or emails

  • Edit and analyze demos through AI tools like Claude and Cursor

Via writenode
