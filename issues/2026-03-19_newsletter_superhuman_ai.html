FROM THE FRONTIER

The duality of AI: thoughts from 80,000 Claude users

Made with Midjourney

Anthropic’s sentiment study. Anthropic just published one of the largest qualitative studies ever on how people experience AI around the globe. The goal: understand how people use it, what they want from it — and what they fear about it.

Conflicted feelings. The answers revealed an interesting trend: people’s wants and fears were often closely tied. Respondents who benefited from AI in a given area regularly voiced concerns about that same benefit — a trend that showed up consistently across regions and subjects.

The data. 33% of respondents said they enjoy using AI to learn new subjects, yet 17% were fearful they’ll eventually stop thinking for themselves. 28% cited economic empowerment as a benefit, while 18% dread job displacement. And people who lean on AI for emotional support? They were 3X more likely to fear becoming dependent on it.

The good news. Benefits tended to be grounded in real experience, while fears were more hypothetical. Either way, it’s clear that people aren't neatly divided into AI optimists and pessimists — they're often both, simultaneously benefiting from AI while worrying about its long-term impacts. How to translate your video ads into any language with AI

  • Check Meta or Google Ads. Find your top 2– 3 non-English markets.

  • Pick one winning English video (use something that already performs well).

  • Go to HeyGen > Translate > Upload your video or paste a link

  • Choose your target language.

  • Keep the default settings and start translation. Wait 5– 10 minutes.

  • Preview the video. Check tone and accuracy. Then download it or copy the share link.

  • Launch it as a new ad. Track results. Repeat for the next market.
