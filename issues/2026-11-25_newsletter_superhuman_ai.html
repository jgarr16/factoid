1. Anthropic makes it easy to build custom apps for your computer: The lab now lets you install artifacts as custom desktop apps on Mac and Windows (learn what an artifact is here). To get started, just ask Claude to build a new artifact, click “Install as app”, and, once you establish privacy boundaries, the final product will appear on your computer. The feature is rolling out now on all plans in the desktop app. See 5 custom productivity apps Anthropic employees have built.

2. Microsoft declares an internal “Code Red” after its November 12th outage: The tech giant is reportedly rushing to overhaul its engineering response practices after a recent outage disrupted Outlook, Teams, and other Microsoft 365 services for about 24 hours. Microsoft reconfirmed this wasn’t a cyberattack but rather a defect in a July update to its gateway servers. VP Amanda Silver compared the situation to a “fog of war” internally.

3. Meta brings Muse into Instagram: You can now prompt Meta’s personal AI assistant directly in Instagram DMs, eliminating the need to toggle between the company’s different apps. You can ask the agent questions about what you see on Instagram, or give it tasks to complete like organizing your saved videos, turning a recipe video into a grocery list, or converting a travel video into an upcoming trip itinerary. It’s rolling out now. Learn more about Muse in Instagram.

How to create a live team dashboard in ChatGPT

Click here to see ChatGPT’s new Space feature in action. Photo: OpenAI

  • Open ChatGPT > ‘Space’, create a new Space, then add a Page or Site for the dashboard.

  • Connect the apps that contain your team data, such as Slack, Google Drive, CRM, project trackers, or other supported tools.

Sample Prompt: “Build a live team dashboard for [team/project]. Track [KPIs], [active projects], [owners], [deadlines], [risks], and [recent updates] using data from [connected apps]. Organize it into a top-level KPI summary, project status table, blockers, and next actions. Keep the layout concise and update the dashboard whenever the underlying information changes.”

  • Review the generated dashboard, then edit the page or Site directly or ask ChatGPT to add charts, filters, status sections, or drill-down views.

  • Share the Space or dashboard page with your teammates so they can view, edit, comment, and ask ChatGPT to make changes from the same shared context.

  • Set the page or Site to update automatically from connected apps and tools so metrics, project status, and team updates stay current.

FROM THE FRONTIER

The B2A economy is getting too big to ignore. Black Friday could be a critical turning point.

Made with Midjourney

We’ve officially entered the Business to Agent Economy (B2A), says venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz. They argue that agents, not humans, are becoming the internet’s primary visitors, which is shifting how money flows online. Other data points agree:

  • Shopify says AI-driven traffic to its stores grew 8x in Q1, while orders from AI-powered searches rose nearly 13x.

  • Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg says shopping is Muse's top use case, with a surprisingly large average purchase size of $200.

As people grow more comfortable sending their agents out on errands, those agents will increasingly influence where money is spent.

Retailers have picked sides. Amazon drew a hard line in September, becoming one of the first major retailers to block Meta’s Muse and other agents. It hasn’t capitulated. Cloudflare has joined in, now blocking shopping agents by default on any page that shows ads. Others, like Walmart, have gone the other way and rolled out the welcome mat.

🦃 The holiday stakes. Adobe expects a record season this year, projecting US online sales to hit $275 billion and AI traffic to rise 130% from last year. Black Friday could mark a major turning point for agentic shopping, especially if online traffic flows meaningfully towards the sites that are letting agents in.
