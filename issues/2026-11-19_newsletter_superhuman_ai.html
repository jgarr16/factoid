How to animate yourself into a reaction GIF with ChatGPT Images

Click here to see ChatGPT Images’ latest feature, which allows it to spin out looping GIFs. Photo: OpenAI

  • Open ChatGPT > Explore > Images, then upload a clear photo of yourself.

  • Ask ChatGPT to animate the uploaded photo into a looping GIF. ChatGPT Images new update allows you to turn a prompt or image into an animated GIF.

Sample Prompt: “Turn me into a seamless reaction GIF showing [reaction]. Keep my face, hairstyle, outfit, and background consistent. Use 6 clear animation beats: neutral > notice > react > exaggerated peak > recover > return smoothly to the first pose. Keep the camera fixed and make the loop seamless.”

  • Specify the reaction, motion intensity, camera framing, and background behavior so only the intended elements move.

  • Open the generated GIF and adjust the playback speed until the reaction feels natural and readable.

  • If the face, pose, or loop drifts, describe the exact change in the same chat and regenerate the GIF with tighter consistency instructions.

FROM THE FRONTIER

A coalition of researchers, founders, and scholars rally against Anthropic right as it goes public

Read The MisAnthropic Statement, a new letter arguing that Anthropic is not a safe company.

Today is Anthropic’s big day. In just five years, the lab has gone from unknown start-up to one of the largest IPOs ever — but not everyone is celebrating. In a new open letter, a coalition of tech and academic leaders argue that Anthropic is not a safe company.

The MisAnthropic Statement claims the lab’s actions don’t match its branding. They argue that Anthropic positions itself as a safe AI company while also chasing recursive self-improvement, which, if achieved, could cause the tech to spiral out of human control. Nearly 90 leaders have signed the statement, including:

  • Thomas Wolf: Co-founder, Hugging Face

  • Igor Babuschkin: Co-founder, xAI

  • Sheldon Lee Glashow: Nobel Laureate in Physics, Harvard

  • Oliver Hart: Nobel Laureate in Economics, Harvard

  • Dan Hendrycks: Executive Director, Center for AI Safety

Some facts support the argument. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei penned a personal essay in mid-September, urging frontier labs to pace AI’s progress. Since then, his lab has shipped five new models, including Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2, along with about a dozen new products.

Anthropic’s never shied away from its risks, though. The lab dedicated more than 90 pages of its IPO filing to potential risk factors, roughly double the norm. It also consistently updates a frontier safety roadmap. Now that Anthropic is public, expect its actions to come under even more scrutiny.
