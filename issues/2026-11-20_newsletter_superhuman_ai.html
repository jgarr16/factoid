A new model turns brainwaves into images of whatever you’re focused on: Alljoined, a startup aiming to “decode” human thoughts, revealed AIR-O, a model that can read noninvasive brain signals and generate an image of the object a person is focusing on in real time. How to build docs and whiteboards during a team call with Claude Meetings

Click here to see Claude’s Meeting feature in action. Photo: Anthropic

  • Open Claude > Meetings, connect your Google Calendar, then select the Google Meet you want Claude to join.

  • Click Add Claude; if needed, the meeting host can admit Claude as a visible participant.

  • During the call, say “Claude” followed by your request, or type it into the Google Meet chat.

Sample Prompt: “Claude, create a live project-planning whiteboard from this discussion. Organize ideas into Goals, Decisions, Open Questions, Risks, and Next Steps. Update it as the conversation progresses, and assign owners and deadlines whenever they are explicitly mentioned.”

  • Open the meeting-page link Claude posts in the Meet chat to view the live transcript, internal chat, and every artifact Claude creates

  • After the call, reopen the meeting from Claude > Meetings to access the summary, action items, and artifacts, or ask Claude about the meeting later in a normal chat.
