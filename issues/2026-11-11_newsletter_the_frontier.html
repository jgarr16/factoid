🧠 Anthropic names its price

The Frontier

Tuesday, Nov 10

by Product Hunt

Presented by AWS

📈 Anthropic set its IPO price at $70 to $78 a share and starts trading on November 19. It's selling 825 million shares on Nasdaq as ANT, valuing the company at up to $1.405T, against $965B in May. People familiar with the deal told CNBC the order book is already covered. (CNBC)

💰 OpenAI raised $11B at a $1.25T valuation, short of what it went looking for. In late September Bloomberg reported it was seeking $30B at $1.4T. The round is still open and could reach about $20B, according to a source who spoke to Bloomberg. (TechCrunch)

🗳️ Voters went against data centers in 40 of 41 local ballot measures. The votes ran across 10 states on November 3, and 21 of them were in Ohio. The one exception was Harwood, North Dakota, where voters rejected an extra review for industrial projects. (CNBC)

🗑️ Meta bought Conductor, and Conductor Cloud deletes your data after November 20. The app runs Claude Code, Codex and Cursor agents side by side, each in its own copy of the codebase, and says more than 100,000 people use it. The cloud service goes read-only on the 13th, and exports stay open until the 20th. (SiliconANGLE)

⚡ Anthropic put out Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 and cut Fable's price by 20%. Fable is now $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output, answers up to 30% faster than 5.1, and blocks about 55% fewer harmless requests. By Anthropic's own account, a team of 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours. (The Next Web)
