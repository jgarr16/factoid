[Webinar] Building AI that doesn't break: fallbacks and rate limits

Webinar · Wednesday, October 14 · 10:00 AM PDT

Building AI That Doesn't Break: Fallbacks, Rate Limits & Reliability

Hi John,

Building an AI application is one thing. Keeping it reliable in production is another.

Model outages, rate limits, and unexpected traffic spikes can disrupt even the most sophisticated AI applications. How can you build systems that stay resilient when things don't go as planned?

Join OpenRouter on October 14 at 10:00 AM PDT for a practical webinar exploring how to build reliable AI applications using model fallbacks, rate limit management, and production-ready infrastructure.

We'll look at how to handle model failures, manage traffic spikes, and design systems that maintain performance and availability as your application scales.

Register for the webinar

What we'll cover

• Model Fallbacks: Keep your application running when your primary model fails or becomes unavailable.
• Rate Limits: Handle throttling and traffic spikes without compromising performance.
• Reliability at Scale: Design AI systems that can handle real-world production challenges.
• Best Practices: Practical strategies for building more resilient AI infrastructure with OpenRouter.

Speaker

Travis Groth, Director of Customer Engineering, OpenRouter

Wednesday, October 14 at 10:00 AM PDT, on Zoom.

Join us to learn how to make your AI applications more resilient, reliable, and ready for production.
