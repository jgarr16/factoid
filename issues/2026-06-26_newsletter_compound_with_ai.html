How the Claude Short-Seller Check Works

99% of stocks are not frauds…

But you only need to be trapped once to lose a lot of capital.

That’s why I built this Claude Skill like a small investigation team.

You give it one company.

Claude sends 3 detectives into the stock.

Detective 1: People Check

this one answers a simple question

Can you trust the people running the company?

It looks for past scandals, lawsuits, insider selling, strange related-party deals, and signs of self-dealing.

Because fraud usually starts with people.

Detective 2: Numbers Check

The key question:

Are profits showing up in cash?

Or only on paper?

Detective 3: Real World Check

Does the business exist at the size management claims?

Because a company can tell a beautiful story.

But factories, stores, customers, and cash are harder to fake.

At the end, Claude gives you a short-seller report:

  • What looks strange
  • What needs proof
  • What would make the fraud case stronger
  • What would prove the short thesis wrong
