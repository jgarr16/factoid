FROM THE FRONTIER

Snowflake CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy: AI is an existential threat to many existing companies

Snowflake CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy

"It is a time of maximum disruption, and many, many successful companies are going to disappear." That’s how Snowflake CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy describes AI’s impact on the Tech industry. We spoke with Ramaswamy, who went from running Google's massive ads business to leading the $50B+ data cloud company, about how Snowflake is enabling the next wave of AI applications.

Enterprise software faces a trillion-dollar disruption: Traditional software companies built around time-intensive processes are under threat. Customer support chatbots with access to all product information can now handle tasks that previously required large teams. "If you are in the business of making software for customer support, obviously that has to change a lot because the reason for your software's existence all of a sudden is dramatically lower," he noted.

Memory and context will define the next generation: Unlike stateless models that "act like a day one intern," successful AI systems need to learn from previous interactions and maintain context across conversations.

Start small, win big. Ramaswamy advises starting with modest business goals and building incrementally. "All too big to fail projects fail," he said. Snowflake applies this internally, developing tools like its Raven sales application on smaller, proven AI components.

The future is outcome-driven. Beyond compute power, Ramaswamy sees pricing models shifting toward business impact, rewarding companies that deliver measurable results. For Snowflake, AI isn’t hype — it’s data, governance, and context converging to empower smarter, faster, and more practical applications.

THE AI ACADEMY

How to create and edit Canva designs with ChatGPT

  • Go to Canva and either create a template or select an existing one.

  • Open ChatGPT, click ‘+’ > Connectors > connect your Canva account.

Sample Prompt: “Open my [template name] in Canva. Create 4 personalized variations with custom messages for different occasions: John (promotion at work), Sara (graduation celebration), David (new home purchase), Emily (running her first marathon). Keep the design style consistent with the original template but update colors, text, and small elements to reflect each event’s mood.”

  • ChatGPT will duplicate your template and automatically fill in personalized text for each variation.

  • Open your variations in Canva, polish the layouts, tweak fonts or colors if needed, and then download and share.

PROMPT STATION

Critical Thinking Pre-Mortem

Prompt: I am planning to launch the following strategy/project: [insert strategy/project outline]. Before I move forward, I want you to play the role of a critical thinking partner and run a pre-mortem. Imagine that the strategy has failed six months down the road. Walk me through what went wrong, considering potential risks, resource misalignments, timing issues, dependencies, and team coordination problems.

Focus on identifying the most likely causes of failure from all possible angles (execution, market, internal operations, etc.).

What key areas of risk should I be looking out for and addressing right now?

Suggest preventive measures I can take or things I might have overlooked.

Break down the specific steps that, if overlooked, could derail the project and how I can avoid them.

Please be as detailed as possible, giving me specific examples and possible scenarios.
