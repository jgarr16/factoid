FROM THE FRONTIER

A German defense startup is using AI to turn cockroaches into spies

Made with Midjourney

Cyborg cockroaches. German defense startup SWARM Biotactics’ latest product is straight out of a James Bond movie: intelligence-gathering cockroaches. The company outfits live roaches with electronic backpacks that hold cameras, microphones, and edge AI processors. Then, it steers the bugs by sending low-voltage electrical impulses to their antennae — causing them to turn, slow down, or stop on command.

Nature’s perfect spy. SWARM Biotactics almost exclusively uses Madagascar hissing cockroaches. They’re the perfect spy: strong enough to carry a small camera, stealthy enough to navigate tight spaces undetected, and they don’t require a battery. The best part? Cockroaches reproduce naturally, which means minimal scaling costs.

Roaches in action. These cyborg insects are designed for environments too risky for humans and too tight for robots. Think: battlefield reconnaissance, hazardous industrial inspections, or search-and-rescue operations in collapsed buildings.

The regulation gap. International arms treaties cover humans, robots, and drones — but not insects. There’s currently no framework governing bio-hybrid surveillance systems and no clear boundary between battlefield recon and civilian surveillance. Legally, cyborg cockroaches operate in no-man’s-land.

Article to Video Script Converter

Input
Article: [Paste article]
Target Audience: [Audience]
Video Length: [Minutes]
Tone: [Informative / Friendly / Authority / Entertaining]

Task
Transform the article into a high-retention video script that keeps viewers watching and clearly communicates the key ideas.

Output Structure
1. Hook (First 5– 10 seconds)
A powerful opening that sparks curiosity (question, surprising fact, bold statement).

2. Intro (10– 20 seconds)
Explain what the viewer will learn and why it matters.

3. Main Segments
Break the content into clear segments. For each include:
Segment Title
Narration Script (conversational, simple)
Visual Ideas (B-roll, graphics, animations, text highlights)
Pattern Interrupts (questions, examples, quick shifts to maintain attention)

4. Call to Action
Clear action (subscribe, comment, visit link, etc.).

5. Outro
Quick recap and closing line.
Script Rules
Write in short, punchy sentences
Keep the script visual-first
Avoid jargon
Maintain constant curiosity and momentum
