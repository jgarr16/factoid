Major AI gadgets unveiled at CES

Welcome back, Superhuman. Razer just became the latest company to give AI a physical form. Its vision: a 6-inch holographic avatar that lives on your desk, offers real-time assistance, and keeps you company.

Today: Razer’s avatar impresses at CES, learn how to create stunning infographics with NotebookLM, and get the latest prompts and trending social posts.

TODAY IN AI

Click to watch Razer’s AI desk companion in action. Source: Razer.

1. Razer unveils a holographic AI avatar for your desk: At CES, the gaming company Razer showcased Project AVA: a 5.5-inch holographic companion that sits on your desk. Powered by xAI’s Grok, the avatar can help you with everything from planning your schedule to beating video game levels. Razer also announced Project Motoko, a headset concept that delivers AI assistance through audio, rather than smart glasses. Both projects are part of Razer's $600 million push into AI. Watch Razer’s holographic AI desk companion in action here.

2. Amazon-owned AI wearable company launches new features: Bee is an always-listening AI wearable that helps customers turn life’s conversations into summaries, personal insights, and reminders. At CES, the Amazon-owned company announced four new features: "Actions" that connect to your email and calendar to take action on your behalf and "Daily Insights" that identify behavioral patterns over weeks to track goals or relationship shifts. The company says no audio is ever stored — it's processed in real-time and then deleted.

3. Samsung expected to report 160% jump in operating profit as AI chip shortage drives up prices: Analysts expect the Korean tech giant to post $11.7 billion in Q4 operating profit tomorrow — its highest since 2018 — driven by a severe memory chip shortage as customers scramble to meet AI demand. The shortage has caused memory prices to jump significantly, including a 314% year-over-year increase for DDR5 DRAM prices. Samsung’s situation marks a dramatic turnaround: just over a year ago, CEO Jun Young-hyun publicly apologized to investors for lagging behind the competition.

PROMPT STATION

Interviewer Research

Prompt: I’m interviewing with {person} at {company} for a {role} position.
Here is their LinkedIn profile: [paste link]

Summarize their technical expertise and management experience, highlighting quantifiable achievements (e.g., team size, revenue impact, products shipped, scale, growth metrics).

Extract 2– 3 talking points I can naturally reference to show I’ve done my research.

Identify patterns in their career (e.g., builder vs. scaler, hands-on vs. strategic).

Generate 5 thoughtful, specific interview questions tailored to their background and the {role}, avoiding generic questions.

Keep everything concise, interview-ready, and phrased in natural language I can use verbatim.
