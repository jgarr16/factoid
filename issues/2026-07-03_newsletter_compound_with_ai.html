The best investing ideas often start with a simple observation.

  • “Europe is hot and nobody has AC.”
  • “Markets are crazy and people are trading more.”
  • “3 AI IPOs could suck up $200B. That money has to come from somewhere.”

All of these are common-sense observations.

As investors, our job is to answer 2 questions:

  1. What industries and businesses benefit from this trend?
  2. And what bets are already crowded?

But if you have never studied that industry before, the answer can be painfully long..

And you can guess the solution:

  3. A guide that shows you how the industry works in hours, not days.

By the end of this article, you’ll have the system ready to use every time you see something in the market and wonder: “Who benefits from this?”
