How to make pro infographics with Figma Make

  • Go to Figma, sign in, and open ‘Make’ (or go to Figma Make directly).

  • Upload a reference infographic, write your prompt, and press enter

Sample Prompt: Turn the article below into a single-page, info-dense infographic for LinkedIn (1080×1350). Use my brand palette [#HEX1, #HEX2, #HEX3]. Create a clear hierarchy (H1 title, 3– 5 section headers, concise bullets), simple icons, and a clean grid. Highlight key stats as callouts, include a minimal footer CTA [enter CTA], and keep body text ≤ 14 words per bullet. Here’s the article: [paste text]

  • Wait a few seconds for the first draft

  • Refine with short prompts (e.g., ‘tighten bullets,’ ‘bigger stat callouts) or tweak in the code editor

  • Click ‘Publish’, copy the public URL

  • Open the HTML to Design plugin, paste the URL, and import back into Figma

  • Edit layout, colors, and icons as needed and export as PDF

FROM THE FRONTIER

Anthropic is catching up to OpenAI’s revenue — but both companies are playing a different game

Made with Midjourney

The next frontier for the AI giants isn’t just building smarter models — it’s how they monetize them. While ChatGPT is racking up big numbers on its consumer subscription revenue, Anthropic has reportedly now surpassed OpenAI on API revenue, putting the Claude maker ahead of its rival on business user revenue.

OpenAI is undergoing its Meta-fication. After the runaway success of ChatGPT (which now has over 800 million users), the company is focused on making the chatbot the next big consumer platform, similar to Instagram and Facebook. The comparison isn’t a coincidence — OpenAI has hired former Meta execs Fidji Simo and Kevin Weil to lead its consumer business, along with several other Meta employees.

Meanwhile, Anthropic is taking the opposite path, hiring Cloud veterans to build its enterprise empire. The company is focused on embedding its models deep inside enterprise stacks and developer workflows. The Wall Street Journal thinks this might even be a better business model — consumers change tastes much faster than businesses.

Back to the future. Meta built the apps everyone spends time on; Amazon built the cloud everything runs on. Both became trillion-dollar companies. OpenAI is betting AI follows Meta’s path: a consumer gateway to the internet. Anthropic's betting it follows Amazon's: invisible infrastructure powering everything. If history's any guide, they're both probably right.
