  • Go to Plugins and connect tools like Gmail, Drive, Slack, Salesforce, and Granola

  • Click the “+” icon in the top-left corner → Create new Bot

  • Describe the Bot’s role and workflow in plain language

  • Create a Chief of Staff Bot for meetings, emails, and follow-ups

“Sample Prompt: Manage my meeting prep, inbox, and follow-ups. Keep updates concise and draft responses in my writing style without sending them.

Or check here for more detailed prompts“ —
